# CS-E 1010 — Fuel Venting

> [!summary]
> The engine design must comply with the fuel venting specifications of CS 34.1.
> Where those specifications are directed at the aircraft rather than the engine,
> the engine must instead incorporate provisions that enable the aircraft it is
> installed in to comply. The obligation therefore splits by who the underlying
> specification addresses.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| — | Design the turbine engine to comply with the fuel venting specifications of CS 34.1. | Required |
| — | Where the imposed specifications are directed at the aircraft, incorporate provisions enabling the aircraft in which the engine is intended to be installed to comply with those specifications. | Required |

CS-E 1010 is a single unnumbered sentence, so the `Ref` cells carry no sub-point
identifier.

The paragraph has no AMC of its own, and carries no amendment marking.

The two limbs are alternatives applied specification by specification, not a
choice for the applicant. Where CS 34.1 addresses the engine, the engine
complies. Where it addresses the aircraft, the engine carries the provisions that
let the aircraft comply, which makes those provisions installation information.

All the substance sits in CS 34.1. CS-E 1010 states no venting limit, no
condition and no test of its own.

## Compliance

- Determination of which CS 34.1 fuel venting specifications address the engine and which address the aircraft [CS-E 1010].
- Design compliance evidence for the engine-directed specifications [CS-E 1010].
- Definition of the provisions enabling aircraft compliance for the aircraft-directed specifications [CS-E 1010], carried into the instructions for installation of [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]] and the assumptions of [[CS-E 30]].
- TCDS note recording compliance, as required by [[CS-E 1000]].

## Application to this engine

The paragraph applies, subject to [[CS-E 1000]]: compliance "may be mandatory for
Engine type certification depending on the specifications referenced under
CS-34" [CS-E 1000].

Fuel venting provisions are part of the fuel system, so the design work meets
[[CS-E 560]]. Where drainage is involved, the fire protection duty of
[[CS-E 130|CS-E 130(f)]] applies in parallel: unintentional accumulation of
hazardous quantities of flammable fluid within the engine must be prevented by
draining and venting, and [[AMC E 130|AMC E 130(6)]] covers drain and vent
systems, including the case where a drain would flow a hazardous quantity during
continued rotation after shutdown. CS-E 1010 is an environmental specification
and CS-E 130(f) is a fire specification; the same hardware serves both.

[VERIFY: CS 34.1 is not held in `source/`. The fuel venting specifications, and
which of them address the engine rather than the aircraft, cannot be stated here.
This determines the whole content of CS-E 1010 for this engine.]
