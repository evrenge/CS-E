# CS-E 150 — Tests - General Conduct of Tests

> [!summary]
> This paragraph governs how certification tests are run rather than what they
> prove. Fuel and oil come from the applicant's specified list, and must be
> justified where the choice could affect a result. Only servicing and minor
> repairs are permitted during a test; a major repair or part replacement needs
> agreed penalty testing. Readings taken less than three minutes after a change
> of engine conditions do not count towards performance, and all test bed and
> measuring equipment must be calibrated.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Normally choose the fuel and oil used for all tests from those specified by the applicant. | Required |
| **(a)** | Where it may have relevance to the results of any particular test, justify the actual fuel and oil to be used, including any additives. | Required |
| **(b)** | Permit only servicing and minor repairs during all tests. | Required |
| **(b)** | Major repairs or replacement of parts may be resorted to, provided that the parts in question are subjected to an agreed level of penalty testing. | Permitted |
| **(c)** | Employ no artificial means of increasing the humidity of the ambient air, except where declared by the applicant. | Required |
| **(d)** | Agree the parameters relevant to the purpose of the test, and record them at appropriate times during the test. | Required |
| **(d)** | Where possible, allow engine conditions to stabilise before observations are taken. | Required |
| **(d)** | Do not include observations taken less than 3 minutes after a change of engine conditions in the assessment of performance, unless the rating cannot be used for more than 3 minutes. | Required |
| **(e)** | Check the adjustments made in compliance with CS-E 140(c), and record unintended variations from the original settings, after each test. | Required |
| **(f)** | Appropriately calibrate all test bed equipment and all measuring equipment used for tests. | Required |

The 3-minute rule in (d) carries its own exception: it does not apply "unless
the rating cannot be used for more than 3 minutes" [CS-E 150(d)]. A rating whose
time limit is shorter than the stabilisation window would otherwise be
impossible to assess, so the exclusion is lifted for it.

Sub-point (b) does not forbid a major repair during testing. It attaches a
condition: an agreed level of penalty testing on the parts concerned
[CS-E 150(b)].

Sub-point (e) is the closing half of [[CS-E 140|CS-E 140(c)]]. The settings are
made before the test under CS-E 140(c) and verified after it under CS-E 150(e),
so drift during the test is detected rather than assumed absent.

## Compliance

- Declaration of the fuel and oil specified for tests, with justification of the actual fuel, oil and additives wherever the choice could affect a result [CS-E 150(a)].
- Test log recording servicing and repairs, with the agreed penalty testing where a major repair or part replacement occurred [CS-E 150(b)].
- Statement of any declared artificial humidity increase, or its absence [CS-E 150(c)].
- Agreed parameter list per test, with the recorded values and their times [CS-E 150(d)].
- Evidence that performance observations excluded readings taken within 3 minutes of a condition change, except for ratings limited to 3 minutes or less [CS-E 150(d)].
- Post-test check of the variable device settings of [[CS-E 140|CS-E 140(c)]], with unintended variations recorded [CS-E 150(e)].
- Calibration records for all test bed and measuring equipment [CS-E 150(f)].

## Application to this engine

All six sub-points apply; none is restricted by engine type or control system.

The 3-minute exception in (d) applies to this engine. `engine_profile.md`
declares 30-Second OEI and 2-Minute OEI, and both are ratings that cannot be
used for more than 3 minutes. Observations at those ratings are therefore
assessed without the 3-minute exclusion, which is the only way a 30-second
rating can be measured at all. Continuous OEI and Rated 30-Minute Power are not
affected by the exception and follow the ordinary stabilisation rule.

CS-E 150(a) points to "(See AMC E 150(a))". That AMC is piston-only: its whole
body permits a higher grade fuel or an approved anti-detonant to suppress
detonation during a test representing Maximum Continuous Power at altitude. A
turboshaft does not detonate, so CS-E 150(a) has no applicable accepted means
for this engine and the fuel justification stands on the specification alone.
The cross-reference is rendered as plain text here because the AMC is excluded
from this vault and has no note.

Sub-point (f) reaches the instrumentation accuracy work of
[[CS-E 60|CS-E 60(b)]]. It also reaches [[CS-E 40|CS-E 40(g)]], under which
those accuracy limits must be taken into account when the performance and
operating limitations are determined. A declared rating depends on measurements
whose calibration this sub-point requires.
