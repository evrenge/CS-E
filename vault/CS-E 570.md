# CS-E 570 — Oil System

> [!summary]
> The oil system must function properly in every intended flight attitude and
> operating condition, with design precautions against incorrect fitment of
> closing devices and against objects entering the tank or its outlet.
> Filtration must preclude damage to the engine and its equipment, with blockage
> indication or a by-pass. The system must be adequately vented against foreign
> matter and against ice forming in the vent line. Unless the tank and all
> external oil components are Fireproof, a shut-off means must prevent discharge
> of hazardous quantities of oil.

## Requirement

### Functioning and design precautions

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)(1)** | Design the oil system to ensure its proper functioning under all intended flight attitudes, installation, atmospheric and operating conditions, including oil temperature and expansion factors. | Required |
| **(a)(2)(i)** | Provide design precautions to minimise the possibility of incorrect fitment of the closing device of the oil filling point or any other access point, or to preclude fluid loss in the event of incorrect fitment. | Required |
| **(a)(2)(ii)** | Provide design precautions to prevent entrance into the oil tank, or into any oil tank outlet, of any object that might obstruct the flow of oil through the system. | Required |
| **(a)(3)** | Design tank filler caps to provide an oil tight seal and so that they will not loosen in flight, and mark them with the word 'oil'. | Required |
| **(a)(4)** | Make provision near each oil pressure connection provided for instrumentation so as to limit the loss of fluid in the event of a pipe Failure. | Required |

### Filtration and by-pass

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(b)(1)** | Protect by suitable filters or strainers all parts of the oil system that are not inherently capable of accepting contaminants likely to be present in the oil, or otherwise introduced into the oil system. | Required |
| **(b)(1)** | Give those filters or strainers a degree of filtration sufficient to preclude damage to the engine and engine equipment, and adequate capacity to accommodate contaminants in relation to the specified servicing intervals. | Required |
| **(b)(2)** | If the most critical main oil filter does not incorporate a by-pass, give it provision for appropriate indication to the flight crew of impending blockage. | Required |
| **(c)** | Construct and install each filter or strainer that has a by-pass so that, if the element is completely blocked, the oil will flow through the rest of the system at a rate within the normal operating range of the system. | Required |
| **(c)(1)** | Design any by-pass so that, when it is in operation, previously collected contaminants in the filter or strainer will not enter the engine oil system downstream of it. | Required |
| **(c)(2)** | Provide indication of by-pass operation to permit appropriate maintenance action to be initiated. | Required |
| **(c)(2)** | This indication need not be provided if the maintenance instructions require the same action to be taken following an impending blockage indication of the most critical main oil filter. | Relief |

### Venting, shut-off and tank

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(d)** | Vent the oil system, including the oil tank expansion space, adequately. | Required |
| **(d)** | Locate or protect all atmospheric vents in the oil system to minimise ingress of foreign matter that could affect satisfactory engine functioning. | Required |
| **(d)** | Arrange venting so that condensed water vapour which might freeze and obstruct the line cannot accumulate at any point. | Required |
| **(e)(1)** | Except where the tank, its supports and all oil system components external to the engine casing are Fireproof, provide a means to shut off the oil supply to the engine. | Required |
| **(e)(1)** | Make that means such that, in the event of Failure of any oil system pipe, it will, when operated, prevent the discharge of hazardous quantities of oil. | Required |
| **(f)(1)** | Ensure each un-pressurised oil tank does not leak when subjected to the maximum operating temperature and a differential pressure of 35 kPa. | Required |
| **(f)(2)** | Each oil tank must have, or have provision for, an oil quantity indicator. | Required |
| **(g)(1)** | Declare and substantiate each brand and type of oil to be approved, and the associated limitations. | Required |
| **(g)(2)** | Identify any parameter of the oil specification likely to be critical for engine functioning or durability, so that engine or rig testing using appropriate oil may be conducted where necessary. | Required |

Sub-point (b)(2) and sub-point (c) are alternatives in practice. The most
critical main oil filter either has a by-pass, in which case (c) governs, or it
does not, in which case (b)(2) requires flight crew indication of impending
blockage [CS-E 570(b)(2)].

The relief in (c)(2) is conditional on the maintenance instructions, not on the
hardware. By-pass indication may be omitted only where the instructions call for
the same action as an impending blockage indication would [CS-E 570(c)(2)].

Sub-point (e)(1) is written as an exception. The shut-off means is required
**unless** the tank, its supports and every external oil system component are
Fireproof. Making them all Fireproof removes the duty; anything less reinstates
it [CS-E 570(e)(1)].

The 35 kPa figure in (f)(1) applies to un-pressurised tanks, at maximum
operating temperature [CS-E 570(f)(1)].

"Hazardous quantities" in (e)(1) is not defined here.
[[AMC E 570|AMC E 570(5)]] resolves it to [[AMC E 130|AMC E 130(1)(e)]].

## Compliance

- Oil system functioning substantiation across all intended flight attitudes, installation, atmospheric and operating conditions, including oil temperature and expansion [CS-E 570(a)(1)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 570|AMC E 570(1)]], [[AMC E 570|AMC E 570(2)]].
- Design precautions against incorrect fitment of closing devices, with positive locking on filling and drainage provisions and drainage outside the cowling where oil can be retained [CS-E 570(a)(2)(i)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 570|AMC E 570(3)]].
- Design precautions preventing object entry into the tank or tank outlet [CS-E 570(a)(2)(ii)]. The access points covered include magnetic chip detectors and gearbox access cover plates [AMC E 570(4)].
- Filler cap sealing, anti-loosening and 'oil' marking evidence [CS-E 570(a)(3)].
- Fluid loss limitation provisions at oil pressure instrumentation connections [CS-E 570(a)(4)].
- Filtration substantiation: degree of filtration sufficient to preclude damage, and capacity against the specified servicing intervals [CS-E 570(b)(1)].
- Impending blockage indication to the flight crew where the most critical main oil filter has no by-pass [CS-E 570(b)(2)].
- By-pass design substantiation covering flow rate within the normal operating range, contaminant retention, and by-pass operation indication or the maintenance instruction basis for omitting it [CS-E 570(c)].
- Venting design covering adequacy, foreign matter ingress and freezing water vapour accumulation [CS-E 570(d)].
- Oil shut-off means and its demonstration against discharge of hazardous quantities of oil; or Fireproof substantiation for the tank, its supports and all external oil system components [CS-E 570(e)(1)], against [[CS-E 130]] and using the hazardous quantity of [[AMC E 130|AMC E 130(1)(e)]].
- Un-pressurised tank leak test at maximum operating temperature and 35 kPa differential pressure [CS-E 570(f)(1)].
- Oil quantity indicator, or provision for one [CS-E 570(f)(2)].
- Declared oil brands and types with their limitations, substantiated [CS-E 570(g)(1)].
- Identification of critical oil specification parameters, with engine or rig testing on appropriate oil where necessary [CS-E 570(g)(2)].

## Application to this engine

All sub-points apply except the propeller provisions recorded below.

**Flight attitudes matter more here than on a fixed-wing installation.**
Sub-point (a)(1) requires proper functioning "under all intended flight
attitudes" [CS-E 570(a)(1)], and the attitude and gyroscopic load effects test
is [[CS-E 680]]. A rotorcraft flight envelope includes attitudes and manoeuvre
conditions that bear directly on oil pick-up, scavenge and venting.

**The oil shut-off in (e)(1) carries extra weight because of continued
rotation.** [[AMC E 130]] states the fire case for turbine oil system components
during continued rotation, and its consequence for the Fireproof standard
[AMC E 130(2)(c)]. The mechanism is the subject of [[CS-E 525]] and
[[AMC E 525]].

**Venting against freezing.** Sub-point (d) requires venting arranged so that
condensed water vapour that might freeze and obstruct the line cannot accumulate
anywhere [CS-E 570(d)]. This is an oil system duty distinct from the fuel system
icing provisions of [[CS-E 560|CS-E 560(e)]].

The accepted means for the oil tank expansion space is in
[[AMC E 570|AMC E 570(2)]].

## Not applicable

- **(e)(2)** — the provision that operation of the oil shut-off means must not prevent utilisation of any oil supply intended for Propeller feathering. Qualified in the source by "When applicable". A turboshaft driving a rotorcraft transmission has no propeller, and propeller material is excluded from this vault by scope.
- **(f)(3)**, including **(f)(3)(i)** to **(f)(3)(iii)** — the trapped oil quantity for one feathering operation, the prevention of sludge or foreign matter entering the Propeller feathering system, and the requirement to complete feathering and unfeathering under all normal operating conditions. All three are conditional on a Propeller feathering system that depends on engine oil.
