# CS-E 80 — Equipment

> [!summary]
> Equipment mounted on the engine must not endanger it. Drives and mountings
> must allow safe engine operation and must contain the consequences of
> equipment failure, so that a failed accessory cannot cause a Hazardous Engine
> Effect. The paragraph then splits equipment by who approves it. Equipment that
> is part of the engine type design is approved with the engine. Aircraft
> equipment driven by the engine is accepted subject to three conditions,
> including approval under the aircraft Type Certificate. Equipment with
> high-energy rotors must either contain its debris or demonstrate design
> integrity.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)(1)(i)** | Design the mountings and drives for all equipment installed on the engine to permit safe operation of the engine with the equipment fitted. | Required |
| **(a)(1)(ii)** | Design those mountings and drives so that Failure of equipment will not result in further damage likely to produce a Hazardous Engine Effect. | Required |
| **(a)(2)(i)** | Design and locate the mountings and drives for equipment identified under CS-E 20(c) so as to minimise the possibility of defective equipment necessitating engine shut-down through contamination or major loss of the engine oil supply. | Required |
| **(a)(2)(ii)** | Design and locate them so as to minimise the possibility of defective equipment necessitating engine shut-down through engine malfunctioning from excessive torque, loose parts falling into the engine, or flailing of the drives. | Required |
| **(b)** | Have the equipment identified under CS-E 20(a) approved as an integral part of the engine, meeting the relevant specifications of CS-E. | Required |
| **(b)** | Where the specifications prescribed in Subpart E will not subject the equipment to cycles of operation that adequately represent all the critical conditions affecting its airworthiness expected in service, state in the equipment specification the additional airworthiness specifications for which evidence of compliance will be needed. | Required |
| **(c)(1)** | Equipment identified under CS-E 20(c) will be accepted for use on an engine subject to the equipment meeting the interface specifications identified under CS-E 20(d). | Statement |
| **(c)(2)** | …subject to evidence of satisfactory compliance with CS-E 80(a). | Statement |
| **(c)(3)** | …subject to being approved under the relevant aircraft Type Certificate. | Statement |
| **(d)(1)** | Equipment with high-energy rotors must be such that Failures will not result in significant non-containment of high energy debris. | Required |
| **(d)(2)** | …or that an acceptable level of integrity of the design, including the high energy parts, has been established. | Required |
| **(d)(3)** | …or an appropriate combination of (d)(1) and (d)(2). | Required |

Sub-point (d) is a single obligation with three routes. The equipment must meet
**one** of them; the applicant chooses which, and (d)(3) allows an appropriate
combination of the other two [CS-E 80(d)].

Sub-point (c) is written as a statement of acceptance conditions rather than as a
duty on the applicant — "will be accepted … subject to" [CS-E 80(c)]. The three
conditions are cumulative. Condition (c)(3) places approval outside CS-E
entirely, in the aircraft Type Certificate.

The division between (b) and (c) follows [[CS-E 20]]. Equipment identified under
CS-E 20(a) is part of the engine type design [CS-E 80(b)]. Equipment identified
under CS-E 20(c) is aircraft equipment mounted on, or driven by, the engine
[CS-E 80(c)].

## Compliance

- Drive and mounting design substantiation showing safe engine operation with the equipment fitted, and containment of equipment failure consequences short of a Hazardous Engine Effect [CS-E 80(a)(1)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 80]].
- Failure analysis of equipment drives and mountings covering oil supply contamination and loss, excessive torque, loose parts and drive flailing [CS-E 80(a)(2)], within the safety analysis of [[CS-E 510]].
- Equipment qualification evidence for engine equipment identified under [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(a)]], against the relevant CS-E specifications [CS-E 80(b)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 80]].
- Equipment specification stating the additional airworthiness specifications needed where the Subpart E test cycles do not represent all critical service conditions [CS-E 80(b)].
- Interface specifications for aircraft equipment, published in the instructions for installation under [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]] [CS-E 80(c)(1)].
- Containment or design-integrity evidence for equipment with high-energy rotors [CS-E 80(d)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 80]].

## Application to this engine

All sub-points apply. None is restricted by engine type, rating or control
system [CS-E 80].

Sub-point (b) names "the specifications prescribed in subpart C or E, as
appropriate" [CS-E 80(b)]. Subpart C is the piston engine type substantiation
subpart and is outside the scope of this vault. For this engine the appropriate
subpart is E. The question is therefore whether the Subpart E test programme
subjects each item of equipment to cycles representative of its service
conditions. That programme comprises the calibration, endurance and
environmental tests of [[CS-E 730]], [[CS-E 740]] and the paragraphs that
follow. Where it does not, the gap must be closed by additional specifications
in the equipment specification [CS-E 80(b)].

Sub-point (a)(1)(ii) uses the Hazardous Engine Effect threshold, which is defined
in [[CS-E 15]] and classified in the safety analysis of [[CS-E 510]]
[CS-E 15(c)]. The set of equipment failures that (a)(1)(ii) reaches is therefore
an output of that analysis.

For a full-authority EECS, the control system components mounted on the engine —
alternators, sensors and actuators — are equipment in the sense of this
paragraph. [[AMC E 50|AMC E 50(2)]] states that such components "should be
covered, in addition, under other CS-E paragraphs such as CS-E 80 or CS-E 170, as
appropriate", so CS-E 80 and [[CS-E 170]] apply to them alongside [[CS-E 50]].

[VERIFY: which equipment items are declared under [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(a)]] and
which under [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(c)]]. The split decides, item by item, whether
CS-E 80(b) or CS-E 80(c) governs, and it is not recorded in
