# CS-E 730 — Engine Calibration Test

> [!summary]
> Power calibration curves of the test engine must be established, so that the
> power changes occurring during the endurance test can be identified and the
> end-of-test performance targets confirmed. The curves come either from specific
> tests immediately before and after the endurance test, or from measurements
> taken during its first and final stages. They run up to the highest rated
> powers, with the 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI ratings expressly excluded.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| — | Establish power calibration curves of the test engine, in order to identify the engine power changes that may occur during the endurance test of CS-E 740, and to ensure that performance targets are met at the end of the endurance test as per CS-E 740(h). | Required |
| — | Establish those curves either by specific tests accomplished immediately before and after the endurance test, or by measurements obtained during the first and final stages of the endurance test. | Required |
| — | Take the curves up to the highest rated powers, except for 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI Power ratings. | Required |

CS-E 730 is a single unnumbered sentence, so the `Ref` cells carry no sub-point
identifier.

The paragraph serves two purposes, and Amendment 8 added the second. The first is
comparative — identify the power changes across the endurance test. The second is
absolute — confirm that the end-of-test performance targets of
[[CS-E 740|CS-E 740(h)]] are met.

The two routes are alternatives with different consequences. Specific tests
before and after are separate runs; measurements during the first and final
stages are taken inside the endurance test itself.

The exclusion of the 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI ratings is in the specification
text, not only in the AMC. [[AMC E 730|AMC E 730(3)]] gives the reason: operation
at those ratings "could significantly affect Engine hardware conditions".

## Compliance

- Power calibration curves established before and after the endurance test of [[CS-E 740]], by specific tests or by first and final stage measurements [CS-E 730].
- Identification of the power changes occurring across the endurance test [CS-E 730].
- Confirmation that the end-of-test performance targets of [[CS-E 740|CS-E 740(h)]] are met [CS-E 730].
- Calibration parameter set appropriate to the engine design [CS-E 730]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 730|AMC E 730(1)]].
- Speed range coverage, at least from minimum idle to the normal maximum compatible with the test day ambient condition [CS-E 730]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 730|AMC E 730(2)]].
- Calibration tests with each bleed in operation separately and with all bleeds in operation, where the engine has bleeds [CS-E 730], required by [[CS-E 690|CS-E 690(a)(2)]].
- Performance results corrected to standard conditions [CS-E 730], under [[CS-E 620]] using the formulae of [[AMC E 620]].

## Application to this engine

The paragraph applies, and the OEI carve-out is written for exactly this rating
set.

**The calibration ceiling excludes two of the three declared OEI ratings.**
`engine_profile.md` declares 30-Second OEI, 2-Minute OEI and Continuous OEI. The
specification takes the curves "up to the highest rated powers except for
30-Second and 2-Minute OEI Power ratings" [CS-E 730], so those two are outside
the calibration while Continuous OEI is not.
[[AMC E 730|AMC E 730(3)]] adds the timing: the curves are established "up to the
highest rating to be approved for a duration of more than 2 minutes prior to the
additional endurance test of CS-E 740(c)(3)(iii)". The additional endurance test
is the OEI sequence, so the calibration is taken before the engine is exposed to
it.

The reason matters for interpretation. The exclusion is not because those ratings
are unimportant, but because running at them changes the hardware — which is the
same concern that makes them subject to the usage recording of
[[CS-E 60|CS-E 60(d)]] and the Approved Life cycle accounting of
[[AMC E 515|AMC E 515(6)(b)]].

**Power, not thrust.** The specification is written as "thrust or power
calibration curves". For a turboshaft the power term governs, and the parameters
[[AMC E 730|AMC E 730(1)]] names include Torque and Rotational Speed alongside
Power.

## Not applicable

- Throughout — where the source pairs thrust with power, in any of the forms it uses, only the power term is carried. This engine produces shaft power; the thrust half of each pair has no turboshaft case.

## Amendment history

Amended at Amendment 8. The change inserted a second purpose into the sentence
and inserted nothing else:

- **Before:** "In order to identify the Engine thrust or power changes that may occur during the endurance test of CS-E 740, thrust or power calibration curves of the test Engine must be established…"
- **After:** "In order to identify the Engine thrust or power changes that may occur during the endurance test of CS-E 740, and to ensure that performance targets are met at the end of the endurance test (as per CS-E 740(h)), thrust or power calibration curves of the test Engine must be established…"

The amendment added "and to ensure that performance targets are met at the end of
the endurance test (as per CS-E 740(h))". This changes the obligation. Before, the
calibration answered a comparative question — how much did power change across the
endurance test. It must now also answer an absolute one — are the
[[CS-E 740|CS-E 740(h)]] performance targets met at the end. A calibration that
established the change without establishing the end-state against the targets no
longer satisfies the paragraph.

Nothing was deleted. The OEI exclusion and the two alternative routes are
unchanged.

The paragraph carries `[Amdt. No.: E/1]` and `[Amdt No: E/8]`.
