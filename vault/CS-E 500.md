# CS-E 500 — Functioning

> [!summary]
> Two short specifications open Subpart D. The engine must be free from
> dangerous surge and instability across its whole operating range of ambient and
> running conditions, within the air intake pressure and temperature conditions
> the constructor declares. Every engine must carry an igniter system able to
> start it on the ground and in flight, up to a declared altitude.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Make the engine free from dangerous surge and instability throughout its operating range of ambient and running conditions, within the air intake pressure and temperature conditions declared by the constructor. | Required |
| **(c)** | Equip the engine with an igniter system suitable for starting the engine on the ground and in flight at all altitudes up to a declared altitude. | Required |

Sub-point (b) is marked "[Reserved]" in Amendment 8 and carries no content.

Sub-point (a) is bounded by two declarations rather than by absolute limits. The
operating range is the engine's own, and the air intake pressure and temperature
conditions are declared by the constructor. Both therefore have to be stated
before the freedom from surge means anything, and both become installation
information under [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]] and assumptions under [[CS-E 30]].

Sub-point (c) requires an igniter system suitable for **both** ground and
in-flight starting. The altitude ceiling is declared, not fixed by the rule.

## Compliance

- Declaration of the air intake pressure and temperature conditions, and of the operating range of ambient and running conditions [CS-E 500(a)], carried in the instructions for installation of [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]] and the assumptions of [[CS-E 30]].
- Surge and instability substantiation across that range [CS-E 500(a)]. The supporting tests are the engine bleed conditions of [[CS-E 690]], the excess operating conditions of [[CS-E 700]], and the ingestion and environmental tests of [[CS-E 780]], [[CS-E 790]] and [[CS-E 800]].
- Igniter system definition and its declared altitude ceiling [CS-E 500(c)].
- Ground and in-flight starting evidence [CS-E 500(c)], demonstrated through the starting tests of [[CS-E 750]], the low temperature starting tests of [[CS-E 770]], and the relighting in flight tests of [[CS-E 910]].
- Continuous ignition provisions [CS-E 500(c)], covered by [[CS-E 720]].

## Application to this engine

Both live sub-points apply; neither is restricted by engine type or rating.

Surge margin under (a) has to hold at the declared OEI ratings as well as at the
all-engines-operating ratings. `engine_profile.md` declares 30-Second OEI,
2-Minute OEI and Continuous OEI, and those are part of the operating range that
(a) covers, so freedom from dangerous surge must be shown there too. The
transient behaviour at rapid power demand is governed by the full-authority EECS,
which links (a) to [[CS-E 50]] and the acceleration work of [[CS-E 745]].

Sub-point (e) of [[CS-E 140]] names CS-E 500 as a paragraph whose features may be
supplied by the aircraft installation rather than the engine type design. Where
that happens and engine performance is affected, the feature must still be
represented throughout the engine tests.

CS-E 500 points to "(See AMC E 500)". That AMC is titled "Functioning - Control
of Engines (Turbine Engines for Aeroplanes)" and is aeroplane-only by its own
title, so it is excluded from this vault and has no note. The cross-reference is
rendered as plain text here for that reason, and CS-E 500 has no applicable
accepted means for this engine.
