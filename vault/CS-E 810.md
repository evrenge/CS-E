# CS-E 810 — Compressor and Turbine Blade Failure

> [!summary]
> Any single compressor or turbine blade must be radially contained after
> failure, and the failure must not lead to a Hazardous Engine Effect through
> other damage before shutdown at a rate worse than Extremely Remote. Where the
> safety analysis relies on turbine blades shedding to protect the rotating
> system in over-speed, that shedding must be shown to occur with a reasonable
> margin above the maximum approved speed and below the minimum rotor burst
> speed.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Demonstrate that any single compressor or turbine blade will be radially contained by the engine after Failure. | Required |
| **(a)** | Demonstrate that the blade Failure will not lead to a Hazardous Engine Effect as a result of other damage before engine shutdown, at a rate in excess of that defined as Extremely Remote. | Required |
| **(b)** | Where, in the Failure analysis of CS-E 510, reliance is placed on the shedding of turbine blades in order to protect the rotating system in over-speed conditions, make tests to demonstrate (b)(1) and (b)(2). | Required if claimed |
| **(b)(1)(i)** | The shedding will occur at a speed which provides a reasonable margin above the maximum engine speed to be approved, including the Maximum Engine Overspeed. | Required if claimed |
| **(b)(1)(ii)** | …and below the minimum rotor burst speed. | Required if claimed |
| **(b)(2)** | No Hazardous Engine Effect is likely to arise as a consequence of the blades shedding. | Required if claimed |

The containment duty in (a) is **radial**. Debris released forward, rearward or
otherwise outside the containment structure is not covered by it, and
[[AMC E 510|AMC E 510(3)(d)(iii)]] requires such debris to be assessed separately
against the [[CS-E 510|CS-E 510(a)(3)]] probability objective. The same split
appears in [[AMC E 810|AMC E 810(2)(c)(ii)]].

The second limb of (a) is probabilistic, not absolute. A Hazardous Engine Effect
following a blade failure may be acceptable, provided it occurs at a rate not in
excess of Extremely Remote — a point [[AMC E 810|AMC E 810(2)(c)(ii)]] makes
explicitly, with a warning attached: the effect must still be acceptable under
every other CS-E specification. A blade failure that causes a shaft failure would
be unacceptable under [[CS-E 850|CS-E 850(a)(1)]] or [[CS-E 840|CS-E 840(c)]]
whatever its probability.

Sub-point (b) is conditional on a design choice recorded in the safety analysis.
Where blade shedding is the over-speed protection, it must work inside a window
bounded below by the maximum approved speed and above by the minimum rotor burst
speed.

## Compliance

- Containment test on the complete engine, or on the individual stage with adjacent stators, for each stage judged most critical for containment [CS-E 810(a)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 810|AMC E 810(2)]].
- Running-following-blade-failure test on a complete engine, at the maximum approved rotational speed, until the engine stops or at least 15 seconds elapse [CS-E 810(a)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 810|AMC E 810(3)]].
- Or alternative evidence: blade failure experience with comparable engines, or development blade failures with representative conditions [CS-E 810(a)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 810|AMC E 810(1)(a)]].
- Assessment of other blade failure conditions — different angular position, partial failure, release below maximum speed [CS-E 810(a)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 810|AMC E 810(1)(c)]].
- Long-term effects assessment for blade failures not detected by the declared instrumentation, with flight crew shutdown provisions and manual instructions where relied on [CS-E 810(a)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 810|AMC E 810(1)(d)]], against [[CS-E 60]] and [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]].
- Record of debris size, weight, trajectory and velocity from the containment test, documented in the manuals of [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]] [CS-E 810(a)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 810|AMC E 810(2)(c)(ii)]].
- Blade shedding speed margin tests, where the safety analysis relies on shedding for over-speed protection [CS-E 810(b)].
- Validated engine model correlated against this test [CS-E 810(a)], required by [[AMC E 520|AMC E 520(c)(2)]].

## Application to this engine

Sub-point (a) applies. Sub-point (b) applies only if the safety analysis of
[[CS-E 510]] relies on turbine blade shedding for over-speed protection.

[VERIFY: whether the Failure analysis of CS-E 510 places reliance on the shedding
of turbine blades to protect the rotating system in over-speed conditions.
`engine_profile.md` does not record it, and it decides whether CS-E 810(b) binds.]

CS-E 810 is referenced from three other paragraphs in this vault.
[[CS-E 520|CS-E 520(c)(2)]] requires the out-of-balance and continued rotation
data to be established following blade Failure "as demonstrated in compliance
with CS-E 810", and [[AMC E 520|AMC E 520(c)(2)(3)]] requires the engine model to
be validated against this test.
[[CS-E 800|CS-E 800(g)(2)]] allows the single large bird test to be waived if the
CS-E 810(a) specifications are shown to be more severe. And
[[AMC E 515|AMC E 515(3)(e)(i)]] notes that it may be necessary to limit the
crack size allowed in service to keep the blade containment requirement of
CS-E 810 satisfied.

The 15-second run in [[AMC E 810|AMC E 810(3)(b)(i)]] is the out-of-balance
exposure, and the test runs at the maximum rotational speed to be approved other
than the Maximum Engine Over-speed — which, if declared under [[CS-E 830]], sits
above it.

## Not applicable

- **(c)**, including **(c)(1)** and **(c)(2)** — the composite fan blade provisions: the Extremely Improbable probability objective of less than 10⁻⁹ per engine flight hour for blade retention system Failure, and the lightning strike substantiation. A turboshaft has no fan.

## Amendment history

Amended at Amendment 7. Sub-point (a) was rewritten, and the change is
substantive in both directions.

- **Before:** "It must be demonstrated that any single compressor or turbine blade will be contained after Failure and no Hazardous Engine Effect can arise as a result of other Engine damage likely to occur before Engine shut down following a blade Failure."
- **After:** "It must be demonstrated that any single compressor or turbine blade will be radially contained by the Engine after Failure and that the blade Failure will not lead to a Hazardous Engine Effect as a result of other damage before Engine shutdown at a rate in excess of that defined as Extremely Remote."

Two things changed. Containment is now qualified as **radial**, and attributed to
the engine: the old text required the blade to be "contained" without saying in
which direction. That narrows the containment duty, and the debris released
axially is picked up instead by the probability assessment that
[[AMC E 510|AMC E 510(3)(d)(iii)]] gained at the same amendment.

Second, the absolute prohibition became probabilistic. The old text said "no
Hazardous Engine Effect can arise"; the new text says the blade Failure must not
lead to one "at a rate in excess of that defined as Extremely Remote". A
Hazardous Engine Effect is now admissible if rare enough.

Read with the parallel change to [[CS-E 520|CS-E 520(c)(1)]], which moved from a
consequence test to a radial containment test at the same amendment, Amendment 7
separated a containment duty from a probability duty across three paragraphs:
containment in CS-E 520 and CS-E 810, probability in CS-E 510.

[[AMC E 810]] was amended at the same time and gained the two new general
paragraphs (1)(c) and (1)(d) that make the wider blade failure threat and the
undetected long-term effects explicit.

The paragraph carries `[Amdt No: E/1]`, `[Amdt No: E/5]` and `[Amdt No: E/7]`.
