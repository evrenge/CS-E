# CS-E 820 — Over-torque Test

> [!summary]
> This test applies only where approval of a Maximum Engine Over-torque is sought
> for an engine with a free power turbine. Fifteen minutes are run at the
> over-torque to be approved, in runs of at least 2½ minutes, at the highest
> power turbine speed at which the over-torque can occur in service, with the
> gearbox oil and turbine entry gas temperatures set to defined maxima.
> Afterwards the stripped engine must be satisfactory for continued running.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | If approval of a Maximum Engine Over-torque is sought for an engine incorporating a free power turbine, demonstrate compliance with CS-E 820 by test. | Required if claimed |
| **(a)(1)** | The test may be run, if desired, as part of the endurance test of CS-E 740. | Permitted |
| **(a)(1)** | Alternatively, evidence may be provided from tests of a complete engine, or equivalent testing of individual groups of components. | Permitted |
| **(a)(2)** | On conclusion of such tests, the stripped condition of the engine or individual groups of components must be satisfactory for continued running. | Required if claimed |
| **(b)(1)** | Run a total of 15 minutes at the Maximum Engine Over-torque to be approved. This may be done in separate runs, each being of at least 2½-minutes duration. | Required if claimed |
| **(b)(2)** | Use a power turbine rotational speed equal to the highest speed at which the Maximum Engine Over-torque can occur in service, but not more than the limit speed of Take-off or OEI ratings of a duration longer than 2 minutes. | Required if claimed |
| **(b)(3)** | For engines incorporating a reduction gearbox, use a gearbox oil temperature equal to the maximum temperature at which the Maximum Engine Over-torque could occur in service; for other engines, an oil temperature within the normal operating range. | Required if claimed |
| **(b)(4)** | Use a turbine entry gas temperature equal to the maximum steady state temperature to be approved for use during periods longer than 20 seconds when operating at conditions not associated with 30-Second or 2-Minute OEI Power Ratings. | Required if claimed |
| **(b)(4)** | …unless it can be shown that other testing provides substantiation of the temperature effects when considered in combination with the other parameters identified in CS-E 820(b)(1), (b)(2) and (b)(3). | Relief |

The whole paragraph is elective: it binds only "If approval of a Maximum Engine
Over-torque is sought" [CS-E 820(a)], and only for an engine with a free power
turbine.

Two of the four test conditions are written around the OEI ratings, and both
exclude the short ones. The power turbine speed in (b)(2) is capped at "the limit
speed of Take-off or OEI ratings of a duration longer than 2 minutes", and the
turbine entry gas temperature in (b)(4) is the maximum for periods longer than 20
seconds "at conditions not associated with 30-Second or 2-Minute OEI Power
Ratings". The over-torque test is therefore run below the 30-Second and 2-Minute
OEI conditions, not at them [CS-E 820(b)(2)], [CS-E 820(b)(4)].

The 15 minutes may be split into separate runs, but each run lasts at least
2½ minutes [CS-E 820(b)(1)].

## Compliance

- Declaration of whether a Maximum Engine Over-torque is sought, and its value [CS-E 820(a)].
- Over-torque test totalling 15 minutes in runs of at least 2½ minutes, at the specified power turbine speed, gearbox oil temperature and turbine entry gas temperature [CS-E 820(b)]; within the endurance test of [[CS-E 740]] if desired.
- Determination of the highest power turbine speed at which the over-torque can occur in service, and of the limit speed of Take-off and of the OEI ratings longer than 2 minutes [CS-E 820(b)(2)].
- Substantiation from other testing where the (b)(4) relief on turbine entry gas temperature is used [CS-E 820(b)(4)].
- Strip examination showing the engine or component groups satisfactory for continued running [CS-E 820(a)(2)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 820]].
- Demonstration that the over-torque event does not compromise the ability to reach Rated 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI Power [CS-E 820(a)(2)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 820]].
- The approved Maximum Engine Over-torque declared as an operating limitation under [[CS-E 40|CS-E 40(d)]] and carried into the manuals of [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]].

## Application to this engine

The paragraph is elective, and it binds only an engine "incorporating a free
power turbine" [CS-E 820(a)].

[VERIFY: whether this engine incorporates a free power turbine.
`engine_profile.md` declares the application, the ratings and the systems, not
the engine architecture. CS-E 820(a) applies only to an engine that has one, so
the answer decides whether this paragraph can be elected at all. The same
architecture question is open for CS-E 740(f)(3), CS-E 740(g)(3),
CS-E 740(g)(4) and CS-E 750(d).]

**Declaring an over-torque unlocks a relief elsewhere.**
The usage-counting relief at [[AMC E 60|AMC E 60(d)(5)]] is conditioned on the
limits established under CS-E 820, [[CS-E 830]] and [[CS-E 870]]. Declaring a
Maximum Engine Over-torque is therefore one of the three ways to make that
relief available. Operation within those limits then need not be considered as
usage of the 30-Second/2-Minute OEI Power Ratings, which bears on the OEI cycle
accounting of [[AMC E 515|AMC E 515(6)(b)]]. The relief itself, with its
recording system condition, is stated once, in [[AMC E 60]].

[VERIFY: whether approval of a Maximum Engine Over-torque is sought.
`engine_profile.md` does not record it. The same question is open for
CS-E 830 and CS-E 870, and the three together decide whether the
AMC E 60(d)(5) relief is available.]

**The acceptance criterion is OEI capability, not just serviceability.**
[[AMC E 820]] states that, to comply with CS-E 820(a)(2), it should be shown
"that an over-torque event does not compromise the ability of the Engine to
reach its Rated 30-Second/2-Minute OEI Power". A strip inspection that finds the
engine serviceable is therefore not the whole of the accepted means: the short
OEI ratings should still be reachable afterwards.

**Torque, speed and temperature each have a test paragraph.** CS-E 820 covers
over-torque, [[CS-E 830]] covers over-speed, and [[CS-E 870]] covers exhaust gas
over-temperature.
