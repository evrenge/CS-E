# CS-E 130 — Fire Protection

> [!summary]
> The engine's design, construction and materials must minimise the probability
> that a fire starts or spreads, minimise its effects, and minimise the
> probability of an internal fire causing structural Failure or Hazardous Engine
> Effects. Two protection levels then apply by component type: external lines and
> components carrying flammable fluid must be at least Fire Resistant, while
> flammable fluid tanks, firewalls, and the engine mounting features must be
> Fireproof. Engine control system components in a designated fire zone must be
> at least Fire Resistant, and accumulation of flammable fluid must be prevented
> by draining and venting.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Make the design and construction of the engine, and the materials used, minimise the probability of the occurrence and spread of fire during normal operation and Failure conditions, and minimise the effects of such a fire. | Required |
| **(a)** | Make the design and construction minimise the probability of the occurrence of an internal fire that could result in structural Failure or Hazardous Engine Effects. | Required |
| **(b)** | Make each external line, fitting and other component that contains or conveys flammable fluid during normal engine operation at least Fire Resistant, except as required by CS-E 130(c). | Required |
| **(b)** | Shield or locate components to safeguard against the ignition of leaking flammable fluid. | Required |
| **(c)** | Make tanks that contain flammable fluid, and any associated shut-off means and supports that are part of and attached to the engine, Fireproof either by construction or by protection, unless damage by fire will not cause leakage or spillage of a hazardous quantity of flammable fluid. | Required |
| **(d)(1)** | An engine component designed, constructed and installed to act as a firewall must be Fireproof. | Required |
| **(d)(2)** | …and constructed so that no hazardous quantity of air, fluid or flame can pass around or through the firewall. | Required |
| **(d)(3)** | …and protected against corrosion. | Required |
| **(e)** | In addition to CS-E 130(a) and (b), make engine control system components located in a designated fire zone at least Fire Resistant. | Required |
| **(f)** | Prevent unintentional accumulation of hazardous quantities of flammable fluid within the engine, by draining and venting. | Required |
| **(g)** | Make those features of the engine that form part of the mounting structure or engine attachment points Fireproof, either by construction or by protection. | Required |
| **(g)(1)** | Exemption from (g) where this is not required for the particular aircraft installation. | Relief |
| **(g)(1)** | Where that exemption is taken, declare it in accordance with CS-E 30. | Required |

The three duties in (d) are cumulative, joined by "and" in the source. A firewall
component must be Fireproof **and** constructed against passage around or through
it **and** protected against corrosion.

Sub-point (c) carries its own relief in the main sentence: the Fireproof standard
does not apply where "damage by fire will not cause leakage or spillage of a
hazardous quantity of flammable fluid" [CS-E 130(c)]. That turns on what a
hazardous quantity is, which the specification does not define; [[AMC E 130]]
supplies a working figure.

The (g)(1) relief is not self-certifying. It removes the Fireproof duty only when
the particular aircraft installation does not require it, and it then creates a
declaration duty under [[CS-E 30]], so the assumption becomes visible to the
installer.

"Fire Resistant" and "Fireproof" are defined terms carrying initial capitals, so
under [[CS-E 15|CS-E 15(a)]] their definitions are binding.

[VERIFY: "Fire-resistant" and "Fireproof" are defined in CS-Definitions, which is
not held in `source/`. [[AMC E 130|AMC E 130(1)(d)]] states only that the
definitions "imply that the functioning of the part under fire condition should
not hazard the aircraft". The exposure times associated with each level are given
in AMC E 130 rather than in the definitions available here.]

## Compliance

- Fire hazard assessment of the design, construction and materials, covering occurrence, spread and effects of fire, and internal fire leading to structural Failure or Hazardous Engine Effects [CS-E 130(a)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 130]].
- Classification of every flammable-fluid-conveying component to the Fire Resistant or Fireproof level, with the reasoning [CS-E 130(b)], [CS-E 130(c)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 130|AMC E 130(2)(c)]].
- Fire test evidence, or analysis, against the pass/fail criteria for each component requiring a fire protection evaluation [CS-E 130(b)], [CS-E 130(c)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 130|AMC E 130(2)(d)]].
- Shielding and location substantiation showing leaked flammable fluid is unlikely to reach an ignition source [CS-E 130(b)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 130|AMC E 130(9)]].
- Flammable fluid tank fire test [CS-E 130(c)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 130|AMC E 130(5)]].
- Firewall substantiation covering the Fireproof standard, passage of air, fluid and flame, and corrosion protection [CS-E 130(d)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 130|AMC E 130(8)]].
- Fire Resistant substantiation for engine control system components in designated fire zones [CS-E 130(e)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 130|AMC E 130(4)(d)]], and the fire item in [[AMC E 80|AMC E 80(2)(c)]].
- Drain and vent system design preventing accumulation of hazardous quantities of flammable fluid [CS-E 130(f)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 130|AMC E 130(6)]].
- Mounting structure and attachment point Fireproof substantiation, or the declaration under [[CS-E 30]] where the installation does not require it [CS-E 130(g)].

## Application to this engine

All sub-points apply except the two piston passages recorded below.

Sub-point (e) is the one shaped by the declared configuration. This engine has a
full-authority EECS, so its control system components are numerous and
distributed, and each one located in a designated fire zone must be at least Fire
Resistant. [[AMC E 130|AMC E 130(2)(d)(i)]] sets what that means in a test: the
control system "should not cause a Hazardous Engine Effect while continuing to
operate but should allow or may cause a safe shutdown of the Engine at any time
within the required exposure time period". A safe shutdown during the test is an
acceptable outcome, provided it is maintained to the end of the period.

The oil system deserves separate attention on a turboshaft.
[[AMC E 130|AMC E 130(2)(c)]] notes that oil system components of turbine engines
"may continue to flow oil after the Engine has been shut down because of
continued rotation", so the shutoff logic that justifies a Fire Resistant rating
for fuel components does not transfer to them. This links to [[CS-E 525]] on
continued rotation and to [[CS-E 570|CS-E 570(e)(1)]].

Sub-point (g) interacts with the installation. The Fireproof duty on mounting
features can be relieved only if the particular aircraft installation does not
require it, and that relief must be declared under [[CS-E 30]]. Since the
installation is a rotorcraft whose type is not yet fixed, the declaration route
should be treated as open rather than assumed.

## Not applicable

- **(c)**, second sentence — the relief allowing a Piston Engine integral oil sump below 23.7 litres capacity not to be Fireproof or Fireproof-shielded. Piston engine provision.
- **(g)(2)**, including **(g)(2)(i)** to **(g)(2)(iv)** — the alternative to the Fireproof mounting duty available to a Piston Engine, with its fail-safe mounting structure, 5-minute fire load case, shutdown load evaluation and 15-minute residual strength provisions. The alternative is open to piston engines only, so this engine takes the Fireproof route or the (g)(1) relief.
