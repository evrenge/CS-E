# CS-E 740 — Endurance Tests

> [!summary]
> The endurance test is where the declared ratings are earned. An engine with
> 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI ratings runs the CS-E 740(c)(3) schedule: 25
> six-hour stages, followed by a four-times repeated two-hour sequence that
> exercises both short OEI ratings. The test also fixes the operating
> limitations, since the declared limits are based on the mean values obtained
> during it. Amendment 8 added two performance obligations: at least 100 % of
> rated power throughout, and rated power still available after the test.

### The applicable schedule — CS-E 740(c)(3)(i)

Twenty-five six-hour stages, for an engine with 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI
Power ratings and an associated Continuous OEI Power rating. This schedule must
be complemented by the additional test of CS-E 740(c)(3)(iii).

| Ref | Part | Content | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(c)(3)(i)** | Part 1 | One hour of alternate 5-minute periods at Take-off Power and minimum test bed idle. | Required |
| **(c)(3)(i)** | Part 2(A) | Stages 1 to 15, each of 30 minutes duration at Maximum Continuous Power. | Required |
| **(c)(3)(i)** | Part 2(B) | Stages 16 to 25, each of 30 minutes duration at Take-off Power. | Required |
| **(c)(3)(i)** | Part 3 | One hour at Maximum Continuous Power, followed by one hour at Continuous OEI Power. | Required |
| **(c)(3)(i)** | Part 4 | 2 hours covering the range in 12 approximately equal increments from minimum test bed idle up to, but not including, Maximum Continuous Power. | Required |
| **(c)(3)(i)** | Part 5 | 30 minutes of accelerations and decelerations consisting of 6 cycles from minimum test bed idle to Take-off Power, maintaining Take-off Power for a period of 30 seconds, the remaining time being at minimum test bed idle. | Required |

### The additional test — CS-E 740(c)(3)(iii)

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(c)(3)(iii)** | Perform the following test sequence four times, for a total time of not less than 120 minutes. | Required |
| **(c)(3)(iii)** | If a stop occurs during this test, repeat the interrupted sequence, unless it is shown that the severity of the test is not reduced if it were continued. | Required |

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Part 1 | Three minutes at Take-off Power. |
| Part 2 | Thirty seconds at 30-Second OEI Power. |
| Part 3 | Two minutes at 2-Minute OEI Power. |
| Part 4 | Five minutes at whichever is the greatest of, as applicable, Continuous OEI Power and Maximum Continuous Power, except that during the first test sequence this period must be sixty-five minutes. |
| Part 5 | One minute at 50 percent of Take-off Power. |
| Part 6 | Thirty seconds at 30-Second OEI Power. |
| Part 7 | Two minutes at 2-Minute OEI Power. |
| Part 8 | One minute at flight idle. |

Each sequence applies the 30-Second OEI rating twice and the 2-Minute OEI
rating twice, so the four repetitions give eight applications of each. The
sixty-five minute first-sequence Part 4 is what brings the total to 120 minutes
exactly: four sequences of fifteen minutes, plus the extra sixty minutes in the
first Part 4. The source requires "not less than 120 minutes"
[CS-E 740(c)(3)(iii)].

The two performance obligations added at Amendment 8 sit at opposite ends of the
test. Sub-point (b)(6) governs what the engine must deliver **throughout** — at
least 100 % of the rated value [CS-E 740(b)(6)] — and (h) governs what it must
still deliver **afterwards** [CS-E 740(h)]. Neither existed before Amendment 8.

Sub-point (f) is the reason the endurance test matters beyond durability. The
declared operating limitations of [[CS-E 40|CS-E 40(d)]] are based on the mean
values obtained during the test [CS-E 740(f)]. The test therefore does not
merely verify limits; it sets them.

Sub-point (i)(2)(iii) is a deliberate relaxation of (i)(1). After the additional
endurance test the engine may be worse than (i)(1) would permit and some parts
may be unusable; what must survive is structural integrity
[CS-E 740(i)(2)(iii)].

## Application to this engine

`engine_profile.md` declares 30-Second OEI, 2-Minute OEI and Continuous OEI.
Therefore, **CS-E 740(c)(3)(i) is the applicable schedule** — the variant for
engines with 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI ratings where a Continuous OEI rating is
associated [CS-E 740(c)(3)(i)]. It is complemented by the two-hour additional
test of (c)(3)(iii). The schedule under (c)(3)(ii) does not apply, because
30-Minute OEI is not claimed.

**The additional test is where the short OEI ratings are earned.** Across four
sequences the engine sees eight applications of 30-Second OEI and eight of
2-Minute OEI [CS-E 740(c)(3)(iii)]. CS-E 740(f) then uses the mean values from
those applications to set the operating limitations for both ratings
[CS-E 740(f)]. The 25-stage schedule of (c)(3)(i) does not run either rating, so
the declared 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI limits come out of this sequence and
nowhere else. The Continuous OEI limits come from Part 3 of the 25-stage
schedule.

**Part 4 of the additional sequence simplifies here.** The source reads
"whichever is the greatest of, as applicable, 30-Minute OEI Power, Continuous
OEI Power and Maximum Continuous Power". With 30-Minute OEI not claimed, the
comparison is between Continuous OEI Power and Maximum Continuous Power. The
sixty-five minute first-sequence Part 4 still applies. What does not arise is
the sentence that splits those sixty-five minutes into thirty minutes at
30-Minute OEI Power followed by thirty-five minutes, because that split is
triggered only where the 30-Minute OEI Power is the greatest
[CS-E 740(c)(3)(iii)].

**The free power-turbine provisions are conditional on the architecture.**
Sub-points (f)(3), (g)(3) and (g)(4) are written for free power-turbine engines,
and (g)(4) for a free power-turbine engine for rotorcraft specifically: 10
minutes of Part 4 in **each** stage at the Maximum Power-turbine Speed for
Autorotation [CS-E 740(g)(4)]. Over 25 stages that is 250 minutes of
autorotation-speed running, and it requires the autorotation power-turbine speed
to be declared.

[VERIFY: whether this engine incorporates a free power turbine.
`engine_profile.md` declares the application, the ratings and the systems, not
the engine architecture. CS-E 740(f)(3), (g)(3) and (g)(4) each open with "In
the case of … free power-turbine" wording, so the answer decides whether they
bind. The same question is open for CS-E 750(d) and CS-E 820(a).]

[VERIFY: the Maximum Power-turbine Speed for Autorotation, and the gas generator
conditions that are most critical in that flight configuration, are not recorded
in `engine_profile.md`. CS-E 740(g)(4) makes both necessary inputs to the
endurance schedule.]

[VERIFY: how the 25 hours of additional 30-Minute Power running required by
CS-E 740(c)(2)(i) combines with the CS-E 740(c)(3)(i) schedule. The 25-hour
provision is written inside the (c)(2) schedule, which does not apply to this
engine, while [[AMC E 740|AMC E 740(c)(2)(i)]] refers more generally to "the
overall test normally required by CS-E 740". The rating is claimed under
CS-E 40(b)(4), so the running is required; where it sits in the programme is not
settled by the text.]

**The strip inspection choice is a programme decision.**
[[AMC E 740|AMC E 740(c)(3)]] gives two acceptable procedures: strip after the
150-hour test and rebuild with the same parts before the additional test, or run
the additional test immediately without disassembly [AMC E 740(c)(3)(1)]. The
second is simpler; the first gives a clean baseline but constrains what may be
replaced [CS-E 740(i)(2)(ii)].

**Speed overshoot at the start of the 30-second period is anticipated.**
[[AMC E 740|AMC E 740(c)(3)]] states that, per [[CS-E 50|CS-E 50(j)]], the
engine control should prevent exceedance of the 30-Second OEI speed limitation.
A short overshoot may be necessary to establish the power quickly. This is
acceptable if validated throughout the (c)(3)(iii) test [AMC E 740(c)(3)(2)].
For a full-authority EECS this is a control design point, not only a test
observation.

## Amendment history

Amended at Amendment 8. Two obligations were added and one sub-point was
re-lettered, alongside the insertion of the turbofan alternate endurance test.

**A new (b)(6).** Sub-point (b) previously ended at (5). Amendment 8 added: "The
thrust or power of the Engine achieved throughout the endurance test must be at
least 100 % of the value associated with the particular Engine rating being
tested, in order to substantiate the thrust or power ratings established under
CS-E 40." There was no explicit floor on achieved power during the test before.

**A new (h).** Amendment 8 added "Engine Performance Target after Test
Completion. After completion of the endurance test, the Engine must be able to
provide the rated thrust or power levels without exceeding operating
limitations." This obligation did not exist before.

**Inspection Checks moved from (h) to (i).** The new (h) displaced it. That
re-lettering is why Amendment 8 also corrected the cross-references in
[[AMC E 650|AMC E 650(10)]] and [[AMC E 690]], both of which pointed at
CS-E 740(h) and now point at CS-E 740(i).

The three changes are one theme. [[CS-E 730]] was amended at the same time to
require the calibration to "ensure that performance targets are met at the end
of the endurance test (as per CS-E 740(h))". Together they add a performance
retention obligation that the endurance test did not previously carry: the
engine must hold at least its rated power throughout, must still deliver rated
power at the end, and the calibration must prove it.

Amendment 8 also inserted the whole of (c)(4), Alternate Endurance Testing for
Turbofan Engines, and added the "or an equivalent demonstration in the alternate
endurance test" clauses to (e)(1), (e)(2), (f)(2) and (g)(1). None of that
reaches this engine. Editorial changes in the same amendment replaced "maximum
continuous" with "MCT" in (e), and abbreviated "Exhaust Gas Temperature" to EGT
in (d)(2) and (f)(4)(iii). In (f), the list of operating limitations based on
the endurance test mean values now names EGT in place of "turbine entry
temperature", and adds bleed.

The paragraph carries `[Amdt No: E/1]`, `[Amdt No: E/4]`, `[Amdt No: E/5]` and
`[Amdt No: E/8]`.
