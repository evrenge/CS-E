# CS-E 920 — Over-temperature Test

> [!summary]
> Two tests, not one. Sub-point (a) is new at Amendment 8 and applies to every
> engine: 5 minutes at maximum permissible rotational speed with turbine entry
> gas temperature at least 42 °C above the maximum rating's steady-state limit,
> after which the turbine assembly must be within serviceable limits. Sub-point
> (b) is the older test and applies only to engines with 30-Second and 2-Minute
> OEI Power ratings: 4 minutes at maximum power-on rotor speed, at least 19 °C
> above the 30-Second OEI temperature limit, after which visible distress is
> tolerated provided turbine integrity is maintained.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Run each engine for 5 minutes at maximum permissible rotational speed (rpm) with the turbine entry gas temperature at least 42 °C (75 °F) higher than the maximum rating's steady-state operating limit, excluding maximum values of rpm and gas temperature associated with the 30-second OEI and 2-minute OEI ratings. | Required |
| **(a)** | Following this run, the turbine assembly must be within serviceable limits. | Required |
| **(b)** | In addition to the test requirements in paragraph (a), for engines with 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI Power ratings, run the engine for a period of 4 minutes at the maximum power-on rotor speed with the turbine entry gas temperature at least 19 °C (35 °F) higher than the 30-Second OEI Power rating operating temperature limit. | Required if claimed |
| **(b)** | Following this test, the turbine assembly may exhibit distress beyond the limits for an over-temperature condition, provided the engine is shown by analysis or test or both to maintain the integrity of the turbine assembly. | Relief |

### The two tests compared

| | **(a)** general test | **(b)** OEI test |
|---|---|---|
| Applies to | Every engine | Engines with 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI Power ratings |
| Duration | 5 minutes | 4 minutes |
| Speed | Maximum permissible rotational speed (rpm) | Maximum power-on rotor speed |
| Temperature margin | At least 42 °C (75 °F) | At least 19 °C (35 °F) |
| Datum | The maximum rating's steady-state operating limit, excluding the 30-second OEI and 2-minute OEI maxima | The 30-Second OEI Power rating operating temperature limit |
| Post-test condition | Turbine assembly within serviceable limits | Distress beyond over-temperature limits permitted, provided turbine integrity is maintained |

The two tests are cumulative. Sub-point (b) opens "In addition to the test
requirements in paragraph (a)" [CS-E 920(b)], so an engine declaring the short
OEI ratings runs both.

The exclusion in (a) is what keeps the two tests separate. The datum for the
5-minute test is the maximum rating's steady-state operating limit with the OEI
maxima taken out, so the general test is not silently pulled up to the OEI
temperature. The OEI condition is then tested on its own terms by (b).

The post-test criteria differ in kind, not only in degree. Test (a) ends at a
serviceability judgement. Test (b) accepts distress beyond the over-temperature
limits and replaces the serviceability judgement with an integrity one, shown
"by analysis or test or both" [CS-E 920(b)]. That is the harder standard to
document and the easier one to pass on hardware condition.

## Compliance

- A 5-minute over-temperature run at maximum permissible rotational speed, at the stated margin above the maximum rating's steady-state limit [CS-E 920(a)].
- Determination of the datum turbine entry gas temperature for that margin [CS-E 920(a)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 920|AMC E 920(1)]].
- Serviceable-limit assessment of the turbine assembly after the (a) run [CS-E 920(a)], against the limits published in the manuals of [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]].
- A 4-minute run at maximum power-on rotor speed at the stated margin above the 30-Second OEI temperature limit [CS-E 920(b)]. Accepted means for the speed: [[AMC E 920|AMC E 920(2)]].
- Turbine integrity demonstration after the (b) run, by analysis or test or both, showing no burst, no blade Failure and no other significant Failure [CS-E 920(b)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 920|AMC E 920(2)]].
- Analysis of any Failure that becomes evident, establishing that its cause would not prevent the OEI rating structure being achieved in service [CS-E 920(b)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 920|AMC E 920(2)]].

## Application to this engine

Both tests apply. The engine declares 30-Second OEI and 2-Minute OEI Power, so
[CS-E 920(b)] is triggered, and [CS-E 920(a)] applies regardless of what is
declared.

**Amendment 8 added a test this engine must run.**
Before Amendment 8 the paragraph contained only the OEI test. An applicant
declaring no short OEI ratings ran nothing under CS-E 920. The new (a) closes
that gap and is the more demanding of the two on post-test hardware condition:
serviceable limits, not merely integrity.

**The datum temperature is a deteriorated-engine datum.**
[[AMC E 920|AMC E 920(1)]] addresses the case where the turbine entry gas
temperature at a fixed gas temperature operating limit varies with the engine's
deterioration condition, and concludes that a deteriorated engine condition may
be assumed. That choice sets the absolute temperature both tests are run at.

**Maximum power-on rotor speed may be a transient value.**
[[AMC E 920|AMC E 920(2)]] normally reads it as the steady state rotor speed at
the 30-Second OEI Power rating, but requires the transient rotor speed to be
substituted where transient speed stabilisation exceeds 3 seconds during the
transition to 30-Second OEI power. The engine's transient behaviour therefore
decides the test speed.

**This is the third over-temperature paragraph, and the only mandatory pair.**
[[CS-E 860]] establishes turbine rotor temperatures after a cooling air Failure.
[[CS-E 870]] is the elective exhaust gas over-temperature test. CS-E 920 is
mandatory for every engine in (a) and mandatory for this engine in (b).

[VERIFY: the maximum rating's steady-state operating temperature limit used as
the (a) datum, and the 30-Second OEI Power rating operating temperature limit
used as the (b) datum. Both follow from the rating declaration under CS-E 40 and
are recorded in engine_profile.md as open items.]

[VERIFY: whether transient speed stabilisation during the transition to
30-Second OEI power exceeds 3 seconds. If it does, AMC E 920(2) requires the
transient rotor speed as the (b) test speed rather than the steady state value.]

## Amendment history

Amendment 8 amended CS-E 920. Before the amendment the paragraph had no
sub-points and consisted of a single test, the one that is now (b). The wording
was: "For Engines with 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI Power ratings, the Engine must
be run for a period of 4 minutes at the maximum power-on rotor speed with the
turbine entry gas temperature at least 19 °C higher than the 30-Second OEI Power
rating operating temperature limit." [work/redline/CS_E_920_Amdt8.md]

Three changes were made.

| Change | Effect on obligation |
|---|---|
| The whole of **(a)** is inserted — the 5-minute test at maximum permissible rotational speed, at least 42 °C (75 °F) above the maximum rating's steady-state operating limit, with the turbine assembly to be within serviceable limits afterwards | **New obligation.** An engine that previously ran nothing under CS-E 920 now runs a 5-minute over-temperature test. An engine that already ran the OEI test now runs both. |
| **(b)** gains the opening "In addition to the test requirements in paragraph (a)," and its "For" becomes "for" | Editorial in itself, but it fixes the two tests as cumulative rather than alternative. |
| **(b)** gains the Fahrenheit equivalent "(35 °F)" | No change to obligation. The Celsius value is unchanged at 19 °C. |

The obligation is raised. The existing OEI test is unchanged in every parameter;
the new test is added on top of it.
