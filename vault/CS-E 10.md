# CS-E 10 — Applicability

> [!quote] Rule text
> "The specifications of subparts A, D, E and F apply to Turbine Engines." — CS-E 10(d)

## What it means
CS-E 10(d) is the scope anchor for this whole vault. A turboshaft is a Turbine Engine. So Subparts A, D, E and F apply to it.

Subparts B and C are for Piston Engines [CS-E 10(c)]. They never apply to us.

CS-E covers type certificates and changes to them, under Part 21 [CS-E 10(a)].

## What we must do
- Show compliance against Subparts A, D, E and F. Required [CS-E 10(d)].
- Apply Part 21 for the type certificate itself [CS-E 10(a)].

## Turboshaft note
CS-E 10(b) makes thrust reverser approval part of CS-E when a reverser is fitted. We fit none, so the Engine certification documentation is endorsed to prohibit thrust reverser use. That endorsement is the only trace CS-E 10(b) leaves on our programme.

## Dropped
- CS-E 10(c) — Subparts A, B and C for Piston Engines; we are a Turbine Engine.

## Accepted means
None in Book 2 for CS-E 10 itself. AMC E 10(b) serves CS-E 10(b), which is excluded, so it has no note.
