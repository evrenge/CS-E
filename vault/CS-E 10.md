# CS-E 10 — Applicability

> [!quote] CS-E 10(d)
> "The specifications of subparts A, D, E and F apply to Turbine Engines."

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | CS-E holds the airworthiness specifications for issuing engine type certificates, and changes to them, under Part 21. | Statement of scope |
| **(b)** | CS-E holds the specifications for approving use of the engine with a thrust reverser, where one is fitted. | Statement of scope |
| **(d)** | Subparts A, D, E and F apply to Turbine Engines. | Statement of scope |

This paragraph imposes no test or analysis. It fixes which subparts form the
certification basis, and every applicability decision in this vault derives from
sub-point (d).

## Compliance

- Certification basis identifying Subparts A, D, E and F [CS-E 10(d)], established under Part 21 [CS-E 10(a)].

## Application to this engine

A turboshaft is a Turbine Engine, so Subparts A, D, E and F apply in full.

No thrust reverser is fitted. Under CS-E 10(b), the engine certification
documentation is therefore endorsed to prohibit thrust reverser use. That
endorsement is the only obligation sub-point (b) places on this programme; the
thrust reverser specifications themselves do not apply.

## Not applicable

- **(c)** — Subparts A, B and C for Piston Engines.

## References
