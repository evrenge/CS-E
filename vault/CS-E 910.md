# CS-E 910 — Relighting In Flight

> [!summary]
> One sentence of obligation and one of content. The engine constructor must
> recommend an envelope of conditions for relighting the engine in flight and
> substantiate it by tests or other evidence. The recommendation must state every
> applicable condition, and the paragraph names five by example.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| — | Recommend an envelope of conditions for engine relighting in flight. | Required |
| — | Substantiate the recommended envelope by appropriate tests or other evidence. | Required |
| — | State all the conditions applicable in the recommendation, for example altitude, air speed, engine windmilling rotational speed, whether starter assistance is required, and the recommended drill. | Required |

CS-E 910 has no sub-points. The five conditions are introduced by "e.g.", so the
list is illustrative and not a closed set: "The recommendation must state all the
conditions applicable, e.g. altitude, air speed, Engine windmilling rotational
speed, whether starter assistance is required, the recommended drill."
[CS-E 910] The obligation is "all the conditions applicable", and the five named
are the minimum an applicant would be expected to address.

The word is "recommend", but the obligation is on the applicant and is
mandatory. The engine constructor must produce the envelope; what is
recommendatory is the envelope's status towards the aircraft, not the duty to
establish it.

## Compliance

- A declared relight envelope, with altitude and air speed boundaries [CS-E 910].
- Windmilling rotational speed at the envelope boundaries [CS-E 910].
- A statement of whether starter assistance is required, and where [CS-E 910].
- The recommended relight drill [CS-E 910], carried into the manuals of [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]].
- Substantiation by engine altitude testing or engine flight testing, or other appropriate tests or evidence [CS-E 910]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 910|AMC E 910(2)]].
- Assessment of rapid relight after an in-flight shutdown and of rotor-lock [CS-E 910]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 910|AMC E 910(3)]].

## Application to this engine

The paragraph applies. For a multi-engine rotorcraft the relight envelope is what
the crew uses after an in-flight shutdown, and it is the counterpart at the
engine level to the OEI ratings: the OEI ratings keep the aircraft flying, and
the relight envelope defines when the failed engine can be brought back.

**The envelope is an engine deliverable consumed by the aircraft.**
[[AMC E 910|AMC E 910(1)]] recommends active coordination between the engine
type-certificate applicant and the aircraft type-certificate applicant. The
envelope boundaries are declared as installation information under [[CS-E 30]]
and reach the crew through [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]].

**Starter assistance ties the envelope to the starting system.**
Whether the relight is assisted determines what the starting system must deliver
at altitude, which is assessed under [[CS-E 690]]. Where starter assistance is
required within the envelope, the electrical or pneumatic supply becomes an
installation assumption under [[CS-E 30]].

**The Engine Control System is inside the obligation.**
The relight is commanded and scheduled by the EECS-FADEC, so the relight
capability is part of the control system function assessed under [[CS-E 50]], and
the fuel schedule during relight is within the fuel system work of
[[CS-E 560]].

[VERIFY: the maximum air speed and altitude for which relighting is recommended,
and whether starter assistance is required over any part of the envelope. Neither
is recorded in engine_profile.md. The air speed boundary depends on the target
rotorcraft, which is itself an open item.]
