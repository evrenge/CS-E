# CS-E 750 — Starting Tests

> [!summary]
> Starting is proved inside the endurance test. Twenty-five cold starts and ten
> hot starts are made at evenly distributed intervals, together with ten False
> Starts in which the ignition circuit is disabled and a normal start follows
> once the declared drainage period expires. The total is brought to one hundred
> starts by the end. A free power-turbine rotorcraft engine has an extra
> condition: each normal start is made with the free power-turbine locked,
> followed by three minutes at Ground Idling with it stationary.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Make twenty-five cold starts, that is after the engine has been stopped for not less than two hours, specifically at evenly distributed intervals throughout the endurance test of CS-E 740. | Required |
| **(a)** | Make ten hot starts, that is within 15 minutes of shutting down after the previous running, at evenly distributed intervals throughout the endurance test. | Required |
| **(a)** | Record the time to light up and accelerate to idling conditions. | Required |
| **(b)** | Make ten False Starts, each followed by a normal start immediately on expiry of the declared drainage period, at evenly distributed intervals throughout the endurance test. | Required |
| **(b)** | Simulate failure to start on these occasions by rendering the ignition circuit inoperative. | Required |
| **(b)** | Following each False Start, the combustion chambers, air casings and similar may be drained, by the normal means provided, of any fuel which may have accumulated. | Permitted |
| **(c)** | At the conclusion of the endurance test, make up the number of starts to a total of one hundred. The additional starts necessary may be hot or cold starts. | Required |
| **(c)** | All attempted starts, including those prescribed in CS-E 750(b), must count towards the total, provided that the normal starting cycle is completed. | Required |
| **(d)** | In the case of a free power-turbine engine for Rotorcraft, make each normal start with the free power-turbine locked, followed by a run at Ground Idling Conditions for three minutes with the free power-turbine stationary, in order to simulate the operation of the engine in the rotorcraft with the rotor system locked. | Required |
| **(e)** | Record full details of all starts made throughout the endurance test, including times to light up and accelerate to idling conditions, details of all attempted starts, and the reasons for any Failures. | Required |

The counts are not additive in the way they first appear. Sub-point (a) requires
25 cold and 10 hot starts and (b) requires 10 False Starts each followed by a
normal start; (c) then brings the **total** to one hundred, with all attempted
starts counting provided the normal starting cycle is completed. The balance is
made up after the endurance test, from hot or cold starts as convenient.

The False Start in (b) is a deliberate simulation, not an observed failure: the
ignition circuit is disabled to produce it. What is being tested is the drainage
provision and the engine's tolerance of accumulated fuel, which is why the normal
start follows immediately on expiry of the declared drainage period.

Sub-point (d) is written for this engine type specifically and adds a condition
to **every** normal start, not to a sample.

## Compliance

- Start schedule distributed through the endurance test of [[CS-E 740]], with 25 cold, 10 hot and 10 False Starts at evenly distributed intervals [CS-E 750(a)], [CS-E 750(b)].
- Declared drainage period, and the drainage means provided [CS-E 750(b)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 750]].
- Record bringing the total to one hundred starts [CS-E 750(c)].
- Evidence that each normal start was made with the free power-turbine locked, followed by three minutes at Ground Idling Conditions with it stationary [CS-E 750(d)].
- Start record covering light-up and acceleration times, all attempted starts, and the reasons for any Failures [CS-E 750(a)], [CS-E 750(e)].
- Endurance test schedule varied and supplemented to accommodate these starts [CS-E 750], required by [[CS-E 740|CS-E 740(a)]].

## Application to this engine

All sub-points apply, and (d) is written for a free power-turbine rotorcraft
engine.

**Sub-point (d) simulates a locked rotor.** Starting with the free power-turbine
locked and holding three minutes at Ground Idling with it stationary reproduces
starting the engine in a rotorcraft whose rotor system is locked. It bears on the
gas generator and the power turbine differently from a normal start, since the
power turbine absorbs no work and the gas path conditions differ.

The locked power-turbine condition also connects to [[CS-E 710]]: a rotor locking
device, if incorporated, must permit the flight crew to unlock for a restart
attempt under [[AMC E 710|AMC E 710(4)]], and CS-E 750(d) exercises starting in
the locked state.

**The starts are inside the endurance test, not beside it.**
[[CS-E 740|CS-E 740(a)]] requires the CS-E 740 specifications to be varied and
supplemented as necessary to comply with CS-E 750, so the starting programme
shapes the endurance schedule.

The ignition hardware exercised here is the system required by
[[CS-E 500|CS-E 500(c)]], and the low temperature end of the starting envelope is
[[CS-E 770]].
