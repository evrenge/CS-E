# AMC E 680 — Inclination and Gyroscopic Load Effects

> [!summary]
> One sentence serves CS-E 680. Inclination and gyroscopic load effects will
> normally be proved during flight testing. In certain cases supplementary
> running or rig testing, to an agreed schedule, may be required before flight
> tests can be permitted. The AMC therefore sets flight test as the default
> means and identifies ground testing as a possible precondition for it.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| — | Normally, inclination and gyroscopic load effects will be proved during flight testing. | Statement |
| — | In certain cases, some supplementary running or rig testing, to an agreed schedule, may be required before flight tests can be permitted. | Statement |

AMC E 680 is a single unnumbered sentence, so the `Ref` cells carry no sub-point
identifier.

The order of events is the point. Ground testing, where required, comes
**before** flight testing is permitted, not after it as confirmation. The trigger
for requiring it is not defined, and the schedule is agreed rather than
prescribed.

Because the proof is normally obtained in flight, the engine-level demonstration
depends on an aircraft. That places CS-E 680 among the paragraphs whose closure
may extend beyond engine certification.

## Compliance

- Flight test evidence of inclination and gyroscopic load effects [AMC E 680].
- Agreed schedule for any supplementary running or rig testing, and its results, where such testing is required before flight tests are permitted [AMC E 680].

## Application to this engine

The AMC applies. Nothing in it is restricted by engine type.

Flight testing as the normal means has a consequence for this programme: the
proof depends on a rotorcraft, and the installation is not fixed. Until it is,
the inclination range and manoeuvre set that [[CS-E 680]] must be demonstrated
against remain assumptions under [[CS-E 30]].

The supplementary rig or running route is the one available at engine level. It
connects to the test conduct rules of [[CS-E 140]] and [[CS-E 150]]. It also
connects to the attitude provision of [[CS-E 600|CS-E 600(e)]], which already
requires tests to be made normally in the installed attitude.
