# CS-E 660 — Fuel Pressure and Temperature

> [!summary]
> A substantiation must establish the minimum and maximum fuel pressure and fuel
> temperature limits to be approved for the engine. The details of that
> substantiation must be agreed with the Agency, and may involve rig tests,
> complete engine tests, or both. The paragraph fixes what must be established
> and who agrees the method; it fixes no values.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| — | Make a substantiation to establish the minimum and maximum fuel pressure and fuel temperature limits to be approved for the engine. | Required |
| — | Agree the details of the substantiation, which may involve rig tests or complete engine tests or both, with the Agency. | Required |

CS-E 660 is a single unnumbered paragraph, so the `Ref` cells carry no sub-point
identifier.

Four limits are required, not two: a minimum and a maximum for pressure, and a
minimum and a maximum for temperature [CS-E 660]. All four are approved limits,
so they become operating limitations under [[CS-E 40|CS-E 40(d)]] and
installation information under [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]].

The method is not prescribed. The paragraph names rig tests and complete engine
tests as possibilities and leaves the choice to agreement [CS-E 660]. This
places the burden of proposing an adequate substantiation on the applicant.

## Compliance

- Substantiation establishing the minimum and maximum fuel pressure limits [CS-E 660].
- Substantiation establishing the minimum and maximum fuel temperature limits [CS-E 660].
- Agreement with the Agency on the details of the substantiation, including whether rig tests, complete engine tests or both are used [CS-E 660].
- The resulting limits declared as operating limitations under [[CS-E 40|CS-E 40(d)]] and carried into the instructions for installation of [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]].

## Application to this engine

The paragraph applies. Nothing in it is restricted by engine type or rating
[CS-E 660].

CS-E 660 points to "(See AMC E 660)". That AMC is titled "Fuel Pump Tests
(Turbine Engines for Aeroplanes)" and is aeroplane-only by its own title, so it
is excluded from this vault and has no note. CS-E 660 therefore has no
applicable accepted means for this engine, and the substantiation rests on the
specification and on agreement with the Agency. The cross-reference is rendered
as plain text here for that reason.

The limits established here are the ones the fuel system of [[CS-E 560]] must
work within. [[CS-E 560|CS-E 560(a)(1)]] requires each fuel specification and
"the associated limitations in flow, temperature and pressure that ensure proper
Engine functioning" to be declared and substantiated, so CS-E 560(a)(1) and
CS-E 660 cover overlapping ground from the design side and the test side.

The minimum fuel temperature limit connects to fuel system icing. Where icing
can occur, [[CS-E 560|CS-E 560(e)]] requires continued satisfactory functioning
without flight crew action. As an accepted method, [[AMC E 560|AMC E 560(4)]]
has the applicant document in the engine installation manual the fuel
temperature at the critical conditions for transient fuel icing.
