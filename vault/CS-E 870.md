# CS-E 870 — Exhaust Gas Over-temperature Test

> [!summary]
> The paragraph is elective. It applies only where the applicant wishes to
> establish a Maximum Exhaust Gas Over-temperature limit. Once that limit is
> sought, a 15-minute test at that temperature is mandatory, run at the maximum
> speed to be approved with each significant spool, and the stripped engine must
> then be satisfactory for continued running.

## Requirement

### General

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)(1)** | Where the applicant wishes to establish a Maximum Exhaust Gas Over-temperature limit, show compliance with CS-E 870. | Required if claimed |
| **(a)(2)** | The test may be run, if desired, as part of the endurance test of CS-E 740. | Permitted |
| **(a)(2)** | Alternatively, test evidence may be provided from an engine of the same type. | Permitted |
| **(a)(3)** | On conclusion of the tests, the stripped condition of the engine must be satisfactory for continued running. | Required if claimed |

### Test Conditions

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(b)(1)** | Run a 15-minute period at Maximum Exhaust Gas Over-temperature with each spool of the engine which could be significant to the test, at the maximum speed to be approved, excluding the Maximum Engine Over-speed (20 Second). | Required if claimed |
| **(b)(2)** | Where desired, the test may be made up of separate runs giving a total time of 15 minutes, the time of each individual run being no less than 2½ minutes. | Permitted |

The structure matches [[CS-E 820]] and [[CS-E 830]]: an elective limit, a
15-minute test that may be split into runs of at least 2½ minutes, an option to
fold the test into the [[CS-E 740]] endurance test, and a strip condition
requirement at the end. What differs is the quantity being over-limit: exhaust
gas temperature here, torque in CS-E 820 and rotational speed in CS-E 830. The
alternative evidence route also differs. CS-E 870(a)(2) accepts an engine of the
same type, whereas [CS-E 830(a)] accepts an engine of similar design.

Sub-point (b)(1) carries two conditions at once. The test is run **at** the
Maximum Exhaust Gas Over-temperature **and at** the maximum speed to be approved.
The speed excludes the Maximum Engine Over-speed (20 Second), so the test does
not combine the over-temperature limit with the over-speed transient.

The test is scoped by spool: it is run "with each spool of the Engine which
could be significant to the test" [CS-E 870(b)(1)]. On a free power-turbine
engine, that means the gas generator spool and the power turbine spool are
each assessed for significance. The 15 minutes are run with each spool that is
significant.

## Compliance

- Declaration of whether a Maximum Exhaust Gas Over-temperature limit is sought, and its value [CS-E 870(a)(1)], carried into the operating limitations of [[CS-E 40|CS-E 40(d)]] and the manuals of [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]]. The declaration item is "Maximum Over-temperature transient and time limit" [AMC E 40(d)(r)].
- Identification of which spools could be significant to the test [CS-E 870(b)(1)].
- A 15-minute over-temperature test at the maximum speed to be approved, in a single period or in runs of at least 2½ minutes [CS-E 870(b)].
- Or test evidence from an engine of the same type [CS-E 870(a)(2)].
- Strip examination showing the engine satisfactory for continued running [CS-E 870(a)(3)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 870]].
- Demonstration that the over-temperature event does not compromise the ability to reach Rated 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI Power [CS-E 870(a)(3)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 870]].

## Application to this engine

The paragraph applies if the limit is claimed. It is not a prerequisite for type
certification: an applicant who declares no Maximum Exhaust Gas Over-temperature
does not run this test. Declaring the limit is what makes the test mandatory.

**The three over-limit tests form one group.**
[[CS-E 820]] (over-torque), [[CS-E 830]] (over-speed) and CS-E 870
(over-temperature) share the 15-minute duration, the 2½-minute minimum run, the
endurance-test option and the strip requirement. [[AMC E 820]], [[AMC E 830]] and
[[AMC E 870]] then share one acceptance criterion: the short OEI ratings must
remain achievable. For an engine declaring 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI Power, that
criterion is the reason the group exists.

**TGT is the parameter the crew and the control system act on.**
The Maximum Exhaust Gas Over-temperature is the limit an over-temperature event
is measured against. Its exceedance is what the usage recording of
[[CS-E 60|CS-E 60(d)]] captures. It is also one of the three limits the
usage-counting relief at [[AMC E 60|AMC E 60(d)(5)]] is conditioned on. The
separate question of whether the engine still delivers its OEI ratings after such
an event is what [[AMC E 870]] requires this test to prove.

**A separate paragraph covers the turbine rotor after a cooling Failure.**
[[CS-E 860]] is mandatory and concerns turbine rotor temperatures following a
cooling air supply Failure. CS-E 870 is elective and concerns an exhaust gas
temperature limit the applicant chooses to declare. They are not alternatives.

[VERIFY: whether a Maximum Exhaust Gas Over-temperature limit is claimed. This is
not recorded in engine_profile.md and it decides whether the CS-E 870 test is
required at all.]

The term appears once in CS-E Amendment 8 and is defined nowhere in it. The
applicant reads it as the steady overshoot limit: for a short period the engine
runs above its rated speed limit. That reading is recorded in
`engine_profile.md`, which is applicant input and not CS-E text.

[VERIFY: whether the "Maximum Engine Over-speed (20 Second)" of CS-E 870(b)(1)
is the Maximum Engine Over-speed that [[CS-E 830]] approves, with a duration
qualifier, or a separate limit. CS-E 830 carries no 20-second qualifier, and
CS-E 830(b)(2) uses "periods longer than 20 seconds" for the temperature basis.
Sub-point (b)(1) excludes the 20-second value from the test speed, so the answer
decides what this test is run at. To be agreed with the Agency.]
