# AMC E 1020 — Engine emissions

> [!summary]
> Two points serve CS-E 1020. The first gives the exact wording for the emissions
> note that CS-E 1000 requires in the type certificate data sheet. The second
> draws out what the certification-date reference means: only the type design at
> certification is assessed against CS-34, a production engine's applicable
> standard depends on its date of manufacture, and any later type design change
> must be assessed for its effect on emissions.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(1)** | Use the format given in the AMC for the note referred to in CS-E 1000. | Accepted method |
| **(2)** | Only the type design at time of engine certification will be assessed against CS-34. | Statement |
| **(2)** | The applicable emissions standard for a production engine is dependent on its date of manufacture. | Statement |
| **(2)** | Assess any further change to the type design for its effect on the engine emissions characteristics and on compliance with the specifications of CS-34, to enable demonstration of compliance for each individual engine when required. | Accepted method |

The note format given in (1) is headed "Note x:" and titled "Engine emissions",
with the operative line reading "Engine (type/model) complies with CS-34
amendment (number)." [AMC E 1020(1)]

Point (2) separates two things that are easy to merge. Type design compliance is
assessed once, against CS-34 as in effect at the certification date. An
individual production engine is a different question, answered by its date of
manufacture. The bridge between them is the last sentence: a type design change
must be assessed so that compliance can still be demonstrated for each individual
engine when required.

## Compliance

- TCDS note in the prescribed format, naming the engine type or model and the CS-34 amendment number [AMC E 1020(1)], satisfying the recording duty of [[CS-E 1000]].
- Emissions assessment procedure for subsequent type design changes, covering the effect on emissions characteristics and on CS-34 compliance [AMC E 1020(2)].

## Application to this engine

The AMC applies. Nothing in it is restricted by engine type, rating or control
system.

Point (2) creates a continuing obligation rather than a certification milestone.
Every later type design change has to be assessed for its emissions effect, which
means the assessment procedure has to exist and be maintained after certification
— in the same way that [[AMC E 515|AMC E 515(3)(g)]] says a regular review
may be required of the Approved Life
assumptions to be reviewed after certification.

The note format in (1) requires a CS-34 amendment number, which cannot be
supplied from the sources held.

[VERIFY: the applicable CS-34 amendment number for this certification is needed
to complete the note format of AMC E 1020(1). CS-34 is not held in `source/`.]
