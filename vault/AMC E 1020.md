# AMC E 1020 — Engine emissions

> [!summary]
> Two points serve CS-E 1020. The first gives the exact wording for the emissions
> note that CS-E 1000 requires in the type certificate data sheet. The second
> draws out what the certification-date reference means: only the type design at
> certification is assessed against CS-34, a production engine's applicable
> standard depends on its date of manufacture, and any later type design change
> must be assessed for its effect on emissions.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(1)** | Use the format given in the AMC for the note referred to in CS-E 1000. | Accepted method |
| **(2)** | Only the type design at time of engine certification will be assessed against CS-34. | Statement |
| **(2)** | The applicable emissions standard for a production engine is dependent on its date of manufacture. | Statement |
| **(2)** | Assess any further change to the type design for its effect on the engine emissions characteristics and on compliance with the specifications of CS-34, to enable demonstration of compliance for each individual engine when required. | Accepted method |

The note format given in (1) is headed "Note x:" and titled "Engine emissions",
with the operative line reading "Engine (type/model) complies with CS-34
amendment (number)." [AMC E 1020(1)]

Point (2) separates two things that are easy to merge. Type design compliance is
assessed once, against CS-34 as in effect at the certification date. An
individual production engine is a different question, answered by its date of
manufacture. The bridge between them is the last sentence: a type design change
must be assessed so that compliance can still be demonstrated for each individual
engine when required.

## Compliance

- TCDS note in the prescribed format, naming the engine type or model and the CS-34 amendment number [AMC E 1020(1)]. This is the note [[CS-E 1000]] states compliance with Subpart F will be recorded in [CS-E 1000].
- Emissions assessment procedure for subsequent type design changes, covering the effect on emissions characteristics and on CS-34 compliance [AMC E 1020(2)].

## Application to this engine

The AMC applies. Nothing in it is restricted by engine type, rating or control
system.

Point (2) creates a continuing obligation rather than a certification milestone.
Every later type design change has to be assessed for its emissions effect. This
means the assessment procedure has to exist and be maintained after
certification. [[AMC E 515|AMC E 515(3)(g)]] describes a similar continuing
duty: a regular review of the assumptions made when establishing the Approved
Life may be required, depending on how conservative those assumptions were.

The note format in (1) requires a CS-34 amendment number, and that is now a
conflict rather than a missing input. EASA repealed CS-34 in May 2025, and
the repeal decision states that "Current references to CS-34, CS-36 and CS-CO2
in type-certificate data sheets (TCDSs) and type-certificate data sheets for
noise (TCDSNs) do not constitute records of compliance with the applicable
environmental protection requirements" [ext ED Decision 2025/005/R]. Point
21.A.41 of Part 21 says what the data sheet must carry instead: "the engine
type-certificate data sheet shall include the record of exhaust emissions
compliance" [ext 21.A.41].

The accepted means in (1) therefore prescribes a note that EASA has since said
is not a compliance record. It remains the published accepted means, and an
applicant may propose an alternative and justify it.

[VERIFY: the wording of the emissions note in the TCDS. AMC E 1020(1) gives a
CS-34 format, ED Decision 2025/005/R says a CS-34 reference is not a record of
compliance, and point 21.A.41 asks for the record of exhaust emissions
compliance. Agree the wording with the Agency before the note is drafted. See
[[CS 34.2]].]

Point (2) is unaffected by the repeal, and it matches the structure of the
requirements behind CS-34: ICAO Annex 16 Volume II Part III sets one standard by
the date of application for a type certificate and another, the production
cut-off standard, by the date of manufacture. [[CS 34.2]] records both.
