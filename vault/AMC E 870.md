# AMC E 870 — Exhaust Gas Over-temperature Test

> [!summary]
> One sentence of AMC serves CS-E 870. It gives the accepted means of showing
> that the stripped engine is satisfactory for continued running, as
> CS-E 870(a)(3) requires. The applicant shows that an over-temperature event
> does not compromise the ability of the engine to reach its Rated
> 30-Second/2-Minute OEI Power. The same criterion appears in AMC E 820 and
> AMC E 830 for the over-torque and over-speed tests.

## Requirement

### AMC E 870(a)(3) — what the strip must establish

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)(3)** | In order to comply with CS-E 870(a)(3), show that an over-temperature event does not compromise the ability of the engine to reach its Rated 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI Power. | Accepted method |

AMC E 870(a)(3) is a single sentence and the only AMC material under CS-E 870.

[[CS-E 870|CS-E 870(a)(3)]] requires the stripped condition of the engine to be
"satisfactory for continued running". This AMC fixes the standard against which
that judgement is made: the short OEI ratings remain achievable after the
over-temperature event [AMC E 870(a)(3)]. A strip that finds every part within
serviceable limits does not meet this accepted means if the rating capability
has been lost.

The wording is parallel to [[AMC E 820]] and [[AMC E 830]], differing only in
the CS reference and the event named — over-torque, over-speed, over-temperature.
The three over-limit tests of Subpart E therefore share one acceptance standard,
expressed in the same sentence three times.

## Compliance

- Demonstration, after the over-temperature test, that Rated 30-Second OEI Power and Rated 2-Minute OEI Power remain achievable [AMC E 870(a)(3)].
- Strip examination findings assessed against that capability, not only against serviceable limits [AMC E 870(a)(3)].

## Application to this engine

The AMC applies wherever [[CS-E 870]] is elected, and it names two of the three
OEI ratings this engine declares. Continuous OEI is not named, in this AMC or in
its two counterparts.

A declared Maximum Exhaust Gas Over-temperature is one of the three limits on
which the usage-counting relief at [[AMC E 60|AMC E 60(d)(5)]] depends. Under
this AMC, a declared limit is supported only if the engine keeps its ability to
reach the short OEI ratings after an over-temperature event [AMC E 870(a)(3)].

The evidence is the engine condition after the 15-minute exposure of
[[CS-E 870|CS-E 870(b)(1)]]. The AMC does not say which damage mechanisms
matter. The strip criteria are therefore established by the applicant and read
together with the Engine Critical Part life substantiation of [[CS-E 515]] and
the turbine rotor temperature work of [[CS-E 860]].
