# AMC E 870 — Exhaust Gas Over-temperature Test

> [!summary]
> One sentence serves CS-E 870, and it is the third instance of a criterion the
> vault has already met twice. Compliance with CS-E 870(a)(3) requires showing
> that an over-temperature event does not compromise the ability of the engine to
> reach its Rated 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI Power.

## Requirement

### AMC E 870(a)(3) — what the strip must establish

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)(3)** | In order to comply with CS-E 870(a)(3), show that an over-temperature event does not compromise the ability of the engine to reach its Rated 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI Power. | Accepted method |

AMC E 870(a)(3) is a single sentence and the only AMC material under CS-E 870.

[[CS-E 870|CS-E 870(a)(3)]] requires the stripped condition of the engine to be
"satisfactory for continued running". This AMC fixes the standard against which
that judgement is made: the short OEI ratings must remain achievable after the
over-temperature event. A strip that finds every part within serviceable limits
does not satisfy the paragraph if the rating capability has been lost.

The wording is identical to [[AMC E 820]] and [[AMC E 830]]. The three over-limit
tests of Subpart E therefore share one acceptance standard, expressed in the same
sentence three times.

## Compliance

- Demonstration, after the over-temperature test, that Rated 30-Second OEI Power and Rated 2-Minute OEI Power remain achievable [AMC E 870(a)(3)].
- Strip examination findings assessed against that capability, not only against serviceable limits [AMC E 870(a)(3)].

## Application to this engine

The AMC applies wherever [[CS-E 870]] is elected, and it names two of the three
OEI ratings this engine declares. Continuous OEI is not named, in this AMC or in
its two counterparts.

The criterion is what links the over-limit tests to the usage recording rules.
[[AMC E 60|AMC E 60(d)(5)]] allows a genuine over-speed, over-torque or
over-temperature event to be excluded from OEI usage counting, provided the
engine can still deliver the OEI ratings. This AMC is where that capability is
proved for the over-temperature case.

Turbine material condition after a 15-minute exposure at the over-temperature
limit is the governing evidence. The hot section damage that would remove
30-Second OEI capability is creep and oxidation of the turbine blades and vanes,
so the strip assessment is read together with the Engine Critical Part life
substantiation of [[CS-E 515]] and the turbine rotor temperature work of
[[CS-E 860]].
