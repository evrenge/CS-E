# CS-E 830 — Maximum Engine Over-speed

> [!summary]
> Where approval of a Maximum Engine Over-speed is sought for a rotating system,
> a test on a complete engine is required, or test evidence from an engine of
> similar design. Fifteen minutes are run at the over-speed to be approved, in
> runs of at least 2.5 minutes, at a defined turbine entry gas temperature and
> the declared maximum operating oil temperature. The stripped engine must then
> be satisfactory for continued running.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | If approval of a Maximum Engine Over-speed is sought for a rotating system of the engine, undertake a test on a complete engine. | Required if claimed |
| **(a)** | Alternatively, test evidence from an engine of similar design may be provided. | Permitted |
| **(b)(1)** | Run a total of 15 minutes at the Maximum Engine Over-speed to be approved. This may be done in separate runs, each being of at least 2.5 minutes duration. | Required if claimed |
| **(b)(2)** | Use a turbine-entry gas temperature equal to the maximum steady state temperature to be approved for use during periods longer than 20 seconds and not associated with 30-Second or 2-Minute OEI Power Ratings. | Required if claimed |
| **(b)(2)** | However, for the shaft system to be approved, if the maximum over-speed cannot occur at the maximum turbine-entry temperature, use the highest temperature which could occur at the conditions of Maximum Engine Over-speed. | Required if claimed |
| **(b)(3)** | Use the declared maximum operating oil temperature. | Required if claimed |
| **(c)** | On conclusion of such tests, the stripped condition of the engine must be satisfactory for continued running. | Required if claimed |
| **(d)** | The test may be run, if desired, as part of the endurance test of CS-E 740, provided the conditions of CS-E 830(b) are satisfied. | Permitted |

The paragraph is elective and is scoped to "a rotating system of the Engine"
[CS-E 830(a)], so an over-speed may be approved for one rotating system without
covering all of them.

Sub-point (b)(2) contains a shaft-specific rule that is easy to miss. The general
condition is the maximum steady state turbine entry temperature for periods
longer than 20 seconds, excluding the short OEI ratings. But for the shaft system,
where the maximum over-speed cannot physically coincide with that temperature,
the temperature used is the highest that could occur **at** the over-speed
condition. The test is made realistic for the shaft rather than artificially
severe.

As in [[CS-E 820|CS-E 820(b)(4)]], the 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI conditions are
excluded from the temperature basis.

Unlike CS-E 820, this paragraph requires a **complete engine** test, with
similar-design evidence as the only alternative; component group testing is not
offered.

## Compliance

- Declaration of whether a Maximum Engine Over-speed is sought, for which rotating system, and its value [CS-E 830(a)].
- Complete engine over-speed test totalling 15 minutes in runs of at least 2.5 minutes, at the specified turbine entry gas temperature and the declared maximum operating oil temperature [CS-E 830(b)]; within the endurance test of [[CS-E 740]] if desired and if the (b) conditions are met.
- Or test evidence from an engine of similar design [CS-E 830(a)].
- Determination of the maximum steady state turbine entry temperature approved for periods longer than 20 seconds outside the short OEI ratings, and, for the shaft system, the highest temperature achievable at the over-speed condition [CS-E 830(b)(2)].
- Declared maximum operating oil temperature [CS-E 830(b)(3)], consistent with [[CS-E 570]] and [[CS-E 740|CS-E 740(f)(4)(vi)]].
- Strip examination showing the engine satisfactory for continued running [CS-E 830(c)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 830]].
- Demonstration that the over-speed event does not compromise the ability to reach Rated 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI Power [CS-E 830(c)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 830]].
- The approved Maximum Engine Over-speed declared as an operating limitation under [[CS-E 40|CS-E 40(d)]] and carried into the manuals of [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]].

## Application to this engine

The paragraph is available and elective.

**A declared over-speed raises the vibration survey ceiling.**
[[CS-E 650|CS-E 650(b)(3)]] makes "100 % of any Maximum Engine Over-speeds
declared under CS E 830" one of the three candidates for the upper survey speed,
and the survey runs to the greatest of them. Declaring an over-speed therefore
adds work in CS-E 650 as well as here.

**It also sits above the blade failure test speed.** Both the containment test and
the out-of-balance run of [[AMC E 810]] are conducted at the maximum rotational
speed to be approved other than the Maximum Engine Over-speed. The source spells
the term both ways: the out-of-balance run at AMC E 810(3)(b)(i) writes "other
than the Maximum Engine Over-speed", while the containment test at
AMC E 810(2)(b)(ii)(A) writes "other than Maximum Engine Overspeed". And
[[CS-E 810|CS-E 810(b)(1)(i)]] requires any blade shedding relied on for
over-speed protection to have a reasonable margin above the Maximum Engine
Over-speed.

**The AMC E 60(d)(5) relief depends on this paragraph.**
The usage-counting relief at [[AMC E 60|AMC E 60(d)(5)]] is conditioned on the
limits established under [[CS-E 820]], CS-E 830 and [[CS-E 870]]. It is
unavailable unless at least one of the three over-limit ratings is declared. The
relief itself is stated once, in [[AMC E 60]].

[VERIFY: whether approval of a Maximum Engine Over-speed is sought, and for which
rotating system. `engine_profile.md` does not record it. The answer affects
CS-E 650(b)(3), the CS-E 810 test margins and the availability of the
AMC E 60(d)(5) relief.]

**The acceptance criterion is OEI capability.** [[AMC E 830]] requires the
demonstration that the over-speed event does not compromise the ability to reach
Rated 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI Power — the same standard [[AMC E 820]] sets for
over-torque.
