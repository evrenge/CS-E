# CS-E 110 — Drawings and Marking of Parts – Assembly of Parts

> [!summary]
> Drawings must fully define the design, the materials by specification, the
> protective and surface finishes, and the tests that establish manufacturing
> quality. Parts must be marked so they can be traced to the drawing they were
> made to, and certain parts, including Engine Critical Parts, need marking plus
> retained records of their manufacturing history. Turbine parts whose incorrect
> assembly could cause Hazardous Engine Effects must be designed against
> misassembly, or permanently marked. Incorrect fitment of instruments, sensors
> and connectors must be assessed in the system safety assessment.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Give full particulars of the design on the drawings for each engine component and each item of equipment, and indicate the materials used in terms of their specifications. | Required |
| **(a)** | Indicate the protective finish and, where applicable, the surface finish. | Required |
| **(a)** | Quote any tests necessary to establish the manufacturing quality of components or equipment on the relevant drawings, either directly or by reference to other suitable documents. | Required |
| **(b)** | Except where otherwise agreed, mark each part so that it can be identified with the drawing to which it was made, and indicate the position of the markings on the drawing. | Required |
| **(c)** | Mark certain parts, including Engine Critical Parts, as may be required by the Agency. | Required |
| **(c)** | Maintain records related to that marking, such that the relevant manufacturing history of the parts can be established. | Required |
| **(d)** | Design turbine engine parts whose incorrect assembly could result in Hazardous Engine Effects so as to minimise the risk of incorrect assembly; or, where that is not practical, mark them permanently to indicate their correct position when assembled. | Required |
| **(e)** | Assess the possibility and subsequent effect of incorrect fitment of instruments, sensors or connectors, as part of the system safety assessment of CS-E 50(d). | Required |
| **(e)** | Where necessary, take design precautions to prevent incorrect configuration of the system. | Required |

Sub-point (d) sets an explicit order of preference. Design against misassembly
comes first. Permanent marking is the fallback, available only "where this is not
practical" [CS-E 110(d)]. Choosing marking without showing that design
prevention was impractical does not satisfy (d) [CS-E 110(d)].

Sub-point (c) names the Agency as the party that determines which parts must be
marked, and points to [[CS-E 515]] for Engine Critical Parts [CS-E 110(c)]. The
records duty is separate from the marking duty, and it falls on the constructor
[CS-E 110(c)].

The exception in (b), "Except where otherwise agreed" [CS-E 110(b)], makes the
marking requirement the default rather than an absolute.

## Compliance

- Drawing set giving full design particulars, material specifications, protective and surface finishes, and the manufacturing quality tests, directly or by reference [CS-E 110(a)]. The material specifications are those qualified under [[CS-E 70|CS-E 70(a)]] and the protective finish is the one required by [[CS-E 90|CS-E 90(a)]].
- Part marking scheme identifying each part with its drawing, with marking positions shown on the drawings [CS-E 110(b)].
- Marking and record-keeping system for Agency-designated parts and Engine Critical Parts, sufficient to establish manufacturing history [CS-E 110(c)], consistent with the Engine Critical Part management of [[CS-E 515]].
- Misassembly analysis for turbine parts whose incorrect assembly could cause Hazardous Engine Effects, with the design provisions taken or the permanent marking applied and the justification for choosing marking [CS-E 110(d)].
- Incorrect-fitment assessment for instruments, sensors and connectors, inside the system safety assessment of [[CS-E 50|CS-E 50(d)]] [CS-E 110(e)].
- Design precautions against incorrect system configuration, where the assessment shows them to be necessary [CS-E 110(e)].

## Application to this engine

All five sub-points apply in full; none is restricted by engine type or rating.

Sub-points (d) and (e) are the two that carry weight for a full-authority EECS.
Sub-point (e) places incorrect fitment of instruments, sensors and connectors
inside the system safety assessment of [[CS-E 50|CS-E 50(d)]], which for this
engine covers the FADEC sensor set. The result also bears on the segregation
of [[CS-E 60|CS-E 60(c)]], which separates the sensors, wiring and signal
conditioning of the monitoring functions from those of the control functions
[CS-E 60(c)].

Sub-point (d) names Hazardous Engine Effects as the threshold, which is defined
in [[CS-E 15]] and classified in the safety analysis of [[CS-E 510]]. The set of
parts that (d) reaches is therefore an output of the safety analysis, not a
separate judgement.

Sub-point (c) covers Engine Critical Parts, whose identification and life
management are specified in [[CS-E 515]]. CS-E 110(c) adds the marking and
manufacturing-history records that make an individual part traceable.
