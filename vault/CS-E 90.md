# CS-E 90 — Prevention of Corrosion and Deterioration

> [!summary]
> Every engine component and item of equipment must be protected from corrosion
> and deterioration in an approved manner. Where a material can make the engine
> self-protecting without corrosion inhibitors, that material must be used
> wherever possible. The paragraph states a design preference, not only a
> protection duty: inhibitor-free material selection comes first, and applied
> protection covers what material choice cannot.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Protect each engine component and each item of equipment from corrosion and deterioration, in an approved manner. | Required |
| **(b)** | Use materials that render the engine inherently self-protecting against corrosion, without internal or external corrosion inhibitors, wherever possible. | Required |

Sub-point (b) is a mandatory preference, not an option. The verb is "must be
used", qualified by "wherever possible" [CS-E 90(b)]. Where an inhibitor-free
material is not used, the applicant carries the justification for why it was not
possible.

"In an approved manner" in (a) means the protection method itself is subject to
agreement, not only its result.

## Compliance

- Corrosion and deterioration protection scheme covering every component and item of equipment, with the protection method identified for each [CS-E 90(a)].
- Material selection rationale showing where inherently self-protecting materials are used, and justification wherever they are not [CS-E 90(b)].
- Protective finish called out on the drawings, as required by [[CS-E 110|CS-E 110(a)]].

## Application to this engine

Both sub-points apply in full; neither is restricted by engine type or rating.

CS-E 90 has no AMC of its own. The nearest accepted means are the environmental
test conditions in [[AMC E 80]]. These cover the salt, fluid and humidity
exposure that equipment must withstand. Also relevant is the material
qualification of [[CS-E 70|CS-E 70(a)]], which establishes durability. The
protection scheme also reaches the continued airworthiness documentation of
[[CS-E 25]], because inspection intervals depend on which protection method was
chosen.
