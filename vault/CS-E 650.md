# CS-E 650 — Vibration Surveys

> [!summary]
> The vibration characteristics of every component that can respond
> mechanically or aerodynamically must be shown acceptable across the declared
> flight envelope, by test or by test combined with validated analysis. The
> surveys run from minimum rotational speed up to a defined maximum that depends
> on the rating period, and extend further if a response is still rising. Flutter,
> scheduled variable geometry, bleed, accessory load and inlet distortion must all
> be covered, and the resulting vibratory stresses must leave justified margins to
> each component's endurance limit.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Establish by test, or a combination of test and validated analysis, that the vibration characteristics of all components that may be subject to mechanically or aerodynamically induced vibratory responses are acceptable throughout the declared flight envelope. | Required |
| **(b)** | Cover in the vibration surveys the ranges of power and rotational speed for each rotor module, corresponding to operations throughout the range of ambient conditions in the declared flight envelope, from the minimum rotational speed up to at least the maximum of (b)(1), (b)(2) and (b)(3). | Required |
| **(b)(1)** | 103 % of the maximum rotational speed permitted for rating periods of two minutes or longer. | Required |
| **(b)(2)** | 100 % of the maximum rotational speed permitted for rating periods of less than two minutes. | Required |
| **(b)(3)** | 100 % of any Maximum Engine Over-speeds declared under CS-E 830. | Required |
| **(c)** | Where there is any indication that a rising response amplitude may lead to peak vibratory stresses occurring above the maximum rotational speed established under CS-E 650(b), extend the surveys sufficiently to reveal the maximum amplitude. | Required |
| **(c)** | The extension need not cover more than a further 2 percentage points increase beyond that speed. | Relief |
| **(d)** | Cover also the aerodynamic and aeromechanical factors which might induce or influence flutter in those systems susceptible to that form of vibration. | Required |
| **(e)** | Evaluate the effects on vibration characteristics of operating with scheduled changes, including allowance for tolerances, to variable vane angles, compressor bleeds, accessory loading, the most adverse inlet airflow distortion pattern declared by the applicant, and the most adverse conditions in the exhaust duct or ducts. | Required |
| **(f)** | Except as provided by CS-E 650(g), ensure that the vibratory stresses determined under CS-E 650, combined with the appropriate steady stresses, provide suitable margins to the endurance limit of each component, after due allowance for operating conditions and for the permitted variations in properties of the associated materials. | Required |
| **(f)** | Justify the suitability of these stress margins for each component. | Required |
| **(f)** | Where it is determined that certain operating conditions or ranges need to be limited, establish operating and installation limitations. | Required |
| **(g)** | Evaluate the effects on vibration characteristics of excitation forces caused by Fault conditions, and show that they do not result in a Hazardous Engine Effect. | Required |
| **(h)** | Substantiate compliance with CS-E 650 for each specific installation configuration that can affect the vibration characteristics of the engine. | Required |
| **(h)** | Where these vibration effects cannot be fully investigated during engine certification, substantiate and define the methods by which they can be evaluated and compliance shown, in the engine instructions for installation required under CS-E 20(d). | Required |

The upper survey speed in (b) is the **maximum** of the three candidates, so all
three are computed and the largest governs. The two rating-period tiers are not
interchangeable: a rating held for two minutes or longer carries a 3 % margin
above its maximum speed, while a rating held for less than two minutes carries
none.

The extension in (c) is triggered by evidence, not by default. It applies only
where there is an indication that the response amplitude is still rising at the
(b) speed, and it is then capped at a further 2 percentage points.

Sub-point (f) is the acceptance criterion for the whole paragraph, and it has
three parts: margins to the endurance limit, justification of those margins per
component, and limitations where a range must be restricted. Sub-point (g) sits
outside (f) by its own wording — a Fault condition is judged against Hazardous
Engine Effect, not against the endurance limit margin.

Sub-point (h) makes the paragraph installation-specific. Where the effects cannot
be closed at engine certification, the **method** must be substantiated and
published, so the work transfers to the installer with a defined route.

## Compliance

- Component selection for the survey, based on experience, analysis and component test [CS-E 650(a)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 650|AMC E 650(3)]].
- Vibration survey test programme covering the speed and power ranges of (b), with any shortfall covered by validated analysis and agreed with the Agency [CS-E 650(b)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 650|AMC E 650(4)]].
- Extended survey where a rising response indicates a peak above the (b) speed [CS-E 650(c)].
- Altitude, temperature and environmental condition coverage, including icing and rain and hail conditions [CS-E 650(a)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 650|AMC E 650(5)]]; the supporting tests are [[CS-E 780]] and [[CS-E 790]].
- Flutter clearance demonstration [CS-E 650(d)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 650|AMC E 650(8)]].
- Evaluation of scheduled variable geometry, compressor bleed, accessory loading, inlet distortion and exhaust duct conditions, with tolerances [CS-E 650(e)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 650|AMC E 650(7)]].
- Stress margin justification per component against the endurance limit, with allowance for material property variation and blade-to-blade frequency scatter [CS-E 650(f)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 650|AMC E 650(9)]].
- Dwell testing on any significant response, normally within the incremental periods of [[CS-E 740|CS-E 740(g)(1)]], followed by the strip inspection of [[CS-E 740|CS-E 740(i)]] [CS-E 650(f)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 650|AMC E 650(10)]].
- Fault condition vibration evaluation against Hazardous Engine Effect [CS-E 650(g)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 650|AMC E 650(6)]].
- Operating and installation limitations where a range must be restricted [CS-E 650(f)], carried into [[CS-E 40|CS-E 40(d)]] and the manuals of [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]].
- Installation compatibility substantiation per configuration, or the published evaluation method where it cannot be closed at engine certification [CS-E 650(h)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 650|AMC E 650(13)]].

## Application to this engine

The paragraph applies in full. Nothing in it is restricted by engine type.

**The rating period split in (b) decides the survey ceiling, and it cuts across
the declared OEI ratings.** `engine_profile.md` declares 30-Second OEI, 2-Minute
OEI and Continuous OEI:

| Rating | Period | Sub-point | Survey speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maximum Continuous Power | continuous | **(b)(1)** | 103 % of its maximum rotational speed |
| Rated 30-Minute Power | 30 minutes | **(b)(1)** | 103 % |
| Take-off Power | 5 minutes | **(b)(1)** | 103 % |
| Continuous OEI | continuous | **(b)(1)** | 103 % |
| 2-Minute OEI | 2 minutes | **(b)(1)** | 103 % — "two minutes or longer" includes exactly two minutes |
| 30-Second OEI | 30 seconds | **(b)(2)** | 100 % |

Only the 30-Second OEI rating falls in the "less than two minutes" tier. The
2-Minute OEI rating sits on the boundary and, by the wording of (b)(1), takes the
103 % margin rather than the 100 % one.

**Maximum Engine Over-speed enters through (b)(3).** If the applicant declares a
Maximum Engine Over-speed under [[CS-E 830]], its 100 % value joins the
comparison and may set the survey ceiling.

[VERIFY: whether a Maximum Engine Over-speed is declared under CS-E 830.
`engine_profile.md` does not record it, and CS-E 650(b)(3) makes it one of the
three candidates for the upper survey speed. The same item is open for
AMC E 60(d)(5).]

**The EECS can lower the required test speed.**
[[AMC E 650|AMC E 650(4)(b)]] states that where it can be demonstrated that the
control system characteristics are such that the maximum rated speed cannot be
exceeded in fault-free operation, the required maximum tested speed may be
adjusted downward with Agency agreement, but never below 100 %. For a
full-authority EECS that is a real option, and it rests on the control system
substantiation of [[CS-E 50]].

**Sub-point (h) is open until the installation is fixed.** Each installation
configuration that can affect vibration must be substantiated, and
[[AMC E 650|AMC E 650(13)]] names the mount system stiffness and damping and the
rotor drive systems among the features to consider. For a rotorcraft the drive
into the transmission is part of that chain.

## Not applicable

- Throughout — where the source pairs thrust with power, in any of the forms it uses, only the power term is carried. This engine produces shaft power; the thrust half of each pair has no turboshaft case.
