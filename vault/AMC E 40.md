# AMC E 40 — Ratings

> [!quote] AMC E 40
> "The thrust and / or power ratings to be approved should be adequately justified by the applicant, using as appropriate the results of the calibration tests (CS-E 350 or CS-E 730) and the values substantiated by the endurance test (CS-E 440 or CS-E 740) or other means."

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| — | Justify the power ratings to be approved, using as appropriate the results of the calibration test and the values substantiated by the endurance test, or other means. | Accepted method |

This is the link between a declared rating and the tests that earn it. A rating
is not established by declaration; it is established by calibration and endurance
evidence.

## Compliance

- Rating justification file drawing on the calibration test results of [[CS-E 730]] [AMC E 40].
- Rating justification drawing on the values substantiated by the endurance test of [[CS-E 740]] [AMC E 40].
- Where neither test is the route, the "other means" proposed and justified [AMC E 40].

## Application to this engine

The turbine engine route applies: [[CS-E 730]] for calibration and [[CS-E 740]]
for endurance.

The declared combination of 30-Second, 2-Minute and Continuous OEI selects the
endurance schedule of CS-E 740(c)(3)(i) with the additional 2-hour test of
CS-E 740(c)(3)(iii). Rated 30-Minute Power adds the provisions of
[[AMC E 740(c)(2)(i)]]. Those are the tests that substantiate the ratings declared
under [[CS-E 40]].

## Not applicable

- CS-E 350 and CS-E 440 — the piston engine calibration and endurance tests, in Subpart C.
