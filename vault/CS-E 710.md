# CS-E 710 — Rotor Locking Tests

> [!summary]
> This test applies only where continued rotation is prevented by a rotor locking
> means. The engine then undergoes 25 locking and unlocking operations under
> defined conditions: shut down from rated Maximum Continuous power, operate the
> locking means as the engine operating instructions specify while subject to the
> maximum torque that continued flight in that condition could produce, and hold
> the rotors stationary for five minutes on each of the 25 operations.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| — | If continued rotation is prevented by a means to lock the rotors, subject the engine to a test that includes 25 locking and unlocking operations of this means. | Required if claimed |
| — | Shut the engine down from rated Maximum Continuous power for the test. | Required if claimed |
| — | Operate the means for stopping and locking the rotors as specified in the engine operating instructions, while being subjected to the maximum torque that could result from continued flight in this condition. | Required if claimed |
| — | Following rotor locking, hold the rotors stationary under these conditions for five minutes, for each of the 25 operations. | Required if claimed |

CS-E 710 is a single unnumbered sentence, so the `Ref` cells carry no sub-point
identifier.

The strength is Required if claimed because the trigger is a design choice. The
paragraph binds only "If continued rotation is prevented by a means to lock the
rotor(s)" [CS-E 710], and [[AMC E 710|AMC E 710(1)]] states plainly what the
choice is for: the applicant "has the option to incorporate a rotor-locking
device into the type design of the Engine in order not to have to comply with
CS-E 525".

The three test conditions are cumulative and each is specific. The shutdown is
from rated Maximum Continuous power, not from idle. The locking means is operated
under the maximum torque continued flight could produce, not unloaded. The hold
is five minutes on **each** of the 25 operations, so the test includes at least
125 minutes of locked holding.

The operating instructions define how the means is operated, which makes the
procedure in the manuals part of the test specification.

## Compliance

- Statement of whether a rotor locking means is incorporated, and therefore whether CS-E 710 or [[CS-E 525]] governs continued rotation [CS-E 710].
- Assessment of the maximum rotational torque that continued flight could produce, considering both damaged and undamaged engine rotors [CS-E 710]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 710|AMC E 710(1)]].
- Test record of 25 locking and unlocking operations, each from a shutdown from rated Maximum Continuous power, under that torque, with a five-minute stationary hold [CS-E 710].
- Operating instructions specifying how the stopping and locking means is operated [CS-E 710], within the manuals of [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]].
- Failure mode assessment of the locking device, including uncommanded or inadvertent activation in flight [AMC E 710(2)], within the safety analysis of [[CS-E 510]]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 710|AMC E 710(2)]].
- Deterioration assessment showing the device will still function when activated despite infrequent use [AMC E 710(3)], against [[CS-E 510|CS-E 510(e)]]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 710|AMC E 710(3)]].
- Design provision for the flight crew to unlock for a restart attempt and to re-lock if unsuccessful [AMC E 710(4)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 710|AMC E 710(4)]].

## Application to this engine

The paragraph applies only if a rotor locking means is incorporated.

The choice is consequential. [[AMC E 710|AMC E 710(1)]] presents the device as
the alternative to complying with [[CS-E 525]]. [[AMC E 525|AMC E 525(1)]]
identifies the mechanism this engine faces: continued rotation on a
multi-engine rotorcraft can be driven by clutch drag, not only by
windmilling. Whether a locking means is worth incorporating therefore depends on
how the free-wheel unit behaves, which is an installation question.

The trade is not one-for-one. [[AMC E 710|AMC E 710(2)]] warns that an engine
with a locking device that continues to rotate because the device failed "might
not satisfy the safety objectives of CS-E 525". The device therefore does not
remove the CS-E 525 concern. It moves that concern into the device's own
Failure modes under [[CS-E 510]].
