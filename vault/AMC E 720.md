# AMC E 720 — Continuous Ignition

> [!summary]
> One sentence serves CS-E 720. It gives three examples of why a continuously
> operated ignition system may be necessary: water or slush ingestion at take-off,
> ice ingestion, or compliance with the icing specifications. The AMC explains
> when the system is needed; it does not add any test obligation of its own.

## Requirement

### AMC E 720(a) — when a continuous system may be necessary

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | The necessity for a continuously-operated system may arise, for example, in consideration of water or slush ingestion at take-off, ice ingestion, or for compliance with the icing specifications. | Statement |

AMC E 720(a) is a single sentence and the only AMC material under CS-E 720.

It imposes no action. Its function is to identify the circumstances that make
continuous ignition necessary, which in turn determines whether
[[CS-E 720|CS-E 720(a)]] binds at all and what
[[CS-E 720|CS-E 720(b)]] means by "the conditions for which their use is
permitted or required".

The three examples are introduced by "for example", so they do not bound the
circumstances.

## Compliance

- Assessment of whether water or slush ingestion at take-off, ice ingestion, or the icing specifications make a continuously operated ignition system necessary for this engine [AMC E 720(a)].
- Where such a system is provided, the conditions for its use, carried into [[CS-E 720|CS-E 720(b)]] and into the operating instructions of [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]].

## Application to this engine

The AMC applies. Nothing in it is restricted by engine type or rating.

Two of the three examples reach this engine directly. Ice ingestion and
compliance with the icing specifications both route to [[CS-E 780]], and the
freedom from dangerous surge and instability that [[CS-E 500|CS-E 500(a)]]
requires has to hold through those conditions. Where continuous ignition is what
makes that possible, CS-E 720 becomes part of the icing compliance argument
rather than an independent paragraph.

Water and slush ingestion at take-off is written for a runway take-off. The
equivalent exposure for a rotorcraft is the rain and hail ingestion of
[[CS-E 790]] and the water spray conditions that arise in operation, so the
assessment is made against this installation rather than against the example as
worded.

The system's hardware obligations sit in [[CS-E 500|CS-E 500(c)]] for the igniter
system itself and in [[CS-E 80]] for the equipment.
