# CS-E 25 — Instructions for Continued Airworthiness

> [!quote] CS-E 25(a)
> "Manual(s) must be established containing instructions for continued airworthiness of the Engine."

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Establish manual(s) containing instructions for continued airworthiness. Update them as existing instructions or the engine definition change. | Required |
| **(b)** | The instructions must contain an airworthiness limitations section, segregated and clearly distinguishable from the rest of the document. For Engine Critical Parts it must also carry any mandatory action or limitation for in-service maintenance and repair identified in the Service Management Plan required under CS-E 515. | Required |
| **(b)(1)** | The airworthiness limitations section must set forth each mandatory replacement time, inspection interval and related procedure required for type certification. | Required |
| **(b)(2)** | For engines having 30-Second OEI and 2-Minute OEI power ratings, the section must also prescribe the mandatory post-flight inspections and maintenance actions associated with any use of either rating. Their adequacy must be validated, and an in-service engine evaluation programme must be established. | Required if claimed |
| **(c)** | Consider the thirteen information items at (c)(1) to (c)(13), as appropriate, for inclusion in the manual(s). | Required |

The obligation at (c) is to **consider** each item; inclusion is "as appropriate".
The obligation at (b) is absolute, and the airworthiness limitations section is
the part that becomes mandatory on the operator.

## Compliance

### Airworthiness limitations section

- Segregated airworthiness limitations section, distinguishable from the rest of the ICA [CS-E 25(b)].
- Mandatory replacement times, inspection intervals and related procedures required for type certification [CS-E 25(b)(1)].
- Mandatory actions and limitations for in-service maintenance and repair of Engine Critical Parts, taken from the Service Management Plan of [[CS-E 515]] [CS-E 25(b)].
- Mandatory post-flight inspections and maintenance actions after any use of 30-Second OEI or 2-Minute OEI Power [CS-E 25(b)(2)].
- Validation of the adequacy of those inspections and actions [CS-E 25(b)(2)].
- In-service engine evaluation programme, assuring the adequacy of the [[CS-E 20]](f) power availability data and of the post-flight instructions. The programme must include service engine tests, or equivalent service engine test experience on engines of similar design, or evaluations of service usage of the 30-Second / 2-Minute OEI ratings [CS-E 25(b)(2)].

### Manual content to consider [CS-E 25(c)]

- (1) Detailed description of the engine, its components, systems and installations.
- (2) Handling instructions, including uncrating, de-inhibiting, acceptance checking, lifting and attaching accessories, with any necessary checks.
- (3) Basic control and operating information: how components, systems and installations operate; methods of starting, running, testing and stopping, with special procedures and limitations.
- (4) Servicing information: servicing points, tank and reservoir capacities, fluid types, system pressures, lubrication points, lubricants and servicing equipment.
- (5) Scheduling information giving the recommended periods for cleaning, inspection, adjustment, test and lubrication, the degree of inspection, serviceability limits and recommended work, with cross-references to the airworthiness limitations section. Where appropriate, an inspection programme stating inspection frequency.
- (6) Troubleshooting information: probable malfunctions, how to recognise them, and remedial action.
- (7) Order and method of engine and part removal and replacement, disassembly and assembly, with precautions. Ground handling, crating and shipping instructions.
- (8) Cleaning and inspection instructions: materials, apparatus, methods and precautions, including methods of inspection.
- (9) Repair methods for worn or non-serviceable parts, information to determine when replacement is necessary, and all relevant fits and clearances.
- (10) Testing instructions, including test equipment and instrumentation.
- (11) Storage preparation instructions, including any storage limits.
- (12) List of tools and equipment necessary for maintenance, with directions for use.
- (13) Instructions applicable to information system security protection as required by CS-E 50(l).

Guidance on the whole ICA: [[AMC E 25]].

## Application to this engine

Sub-point (b)(2) binds. The applicant declares both 30-Second OEI and 2-Minute
OEI, so the mandatory post-flight inspection regime, its validation, and the
in-service evaluation programme are all required.

That regime is the operational cost of the two ratings. Every use of either
rating triggers mandatory post-flight action, and [[AMC E 40(b)(3)]] records that
certification assumes the worst case of three uses in one flight.

Item (c)(13) binds because the engine carries a full-authority EECS: the security
instructions required by [[CS-E 50]](l) must be considered for the manual.

Item (c)(5) uses "should" for the recommended periods. That softer verb describes
the content of the scheduling information; the obligation to consider the item for
inclusion is unaffected.
