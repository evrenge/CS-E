# CS-E 690 — Engine Bleed

> [!summary]
> An engine with bleeds must either vary its endurance test schedule to exercise
> them, or substantiate their use by separate test and analysis. The variation
> has three parts: exercise the bleed controls at the end of each endurance
> stage, run five named stages with the bleeds in operation, and add calibration
> tests with each bleed separately and with all bleeds together. Where bleed air
> is to be declared suitable for cabin pressurisation or ventilation, its purity
> must be tested and a defect analysis prepared.

## Requirement

### Endurance and calibration variation

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | For an engine having bleeds for aircraft or engine uses, vary the engine endurance test schedule of CS-E 740 in accordance with CS-E 690(a), unless the use of the bleeds is substantiated by separate test and analysis. | Required |
| **(a)(1)(i)** | Exercise the bleed controls at the end of each stage of the endurance test as per CS-E 740(c)(1), (2) or (3). | Required |
| **(a)(1)(ii)** | Complete any other tests which may be necessary to demonstrate the satisfactory functioning of the engine and the bleeds. | Required |
| **(a)(1)(iii)** | During the tests of CS-E 690(a)(3), the engine rotational speeds may be reduced if necessary when the bleeds are in operation, in order to avoid exceeding the maximum declared jet pipe temperatures. | Permitted |
| **(a)(2)** | Include a calibration test with each bleed in operation separately, and one with all bleeds in operation. | Required |
| **(a)(3)(i)** | If the applicant uses an endurance test as per CS-E 740(c)(1), (2) or (3), run Stages 3, 7, 13, 17 and 23 with the bleeds in operation during all the conditions of running for which they are intended to be approved for use. | Required |
| **(a)(3)(ii)** | During the four test sequences of CS-E 740(c)(3)(iii), an air bleed extraction need not be used where it is shown that the validity of the test is not compromised. | Relief |

The opening sentence offers a genuine alternative [CS-E 690(a)]. Either the
endurance schedule is varied as (a) sets out, **or** the bleeds are
substantiated by separate test and analysis. Choosing the second route removes
the schedule variation, not the substantiation.

The five named stages — 3, 7, 13, 17 and 23 — are fixed, and the bleeds must be
in operation "during all the conditions of running for which they are intended
to be approved for use" [CS-E 690(a)(3)(i)]. The declared bleed usage therefore
determines what happens inside those stages.

The permission in (a)(1)(iii) protects the jet pipe temperature limit, not the
test objective. Speed may be reduced to stay within the declared maximum, and
the paragraph points to [[CS-E 740|CS-E 740(f)(2)]] [CS-E 690(a)(1)(iii)].

### Bleed air for cabin pressurisation or ventilation

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(b)** | The specifications of CS-E 690(b) are applicable where it is desired to declare that compressor bleed air is suitable for direct use in an aircraft cabin pressurisation or ventilation system. | Statement |
| **(b)(1)** | Make tests to determine the purity of the air supply. | Required if claimed |
| **(b)(2)** | Prepare an analysis of defects which could affect the purity of the bleed air; where necessary simulate the defects and make tests, as agreed by the Agency, to establish the degree of contamination which is likely to occur. | Required if claimed |
| **(b)(2)** | If the defect under consideration is such that the engine would be shut down immediately, the tests required may be modified accordingly. | Permitted |

Sub-point (b) is elective by its own wording: it applies "where it is desired to
declare" the bleed air suitable for direct cabin use [CS-E 690(b)]. The
declaration is optional; the tests become mandatory once it is sought.

## Compliance

- Declaration of the bleeds provided, their aircraft and engine uses, and the conditions of running for which they are to be approved [CS-E 690(a)].
- Endurance schedule variation exercising the bleed controls at each stage end, and running Stages 3, 7, 13, 17 and 23 with bleeds in operation; or the separate test and analysis substantiating bleed use [CS-E 690(a)], within [[CS-E 740]].
- Calibration tests with each bleed separately and with all bleeds together [CS-E 690(a)(2)], within [[CS-E 730]].
- Justification where rotational speed is reduced to stay within the declared maximum jet pipe temperature [CS-E 690(a)(1)(iii)].
- Demonstration that test validity is not compromised, where the (a)(3)(ii) relief is used during the [[CS-E 740|CS-E 740(c)(3)(iii)]] sequences [CS-E 690(a)(3)(ii)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 690]].
- Bleed air purity test results, where direct cabin use is declared [CS-E 690(b)(1)].
- Defect analysis affecting bleed air purity, with simulated defects and agreed tests where necessary [CS-E 690(b)(2)].

## Application to this engine

Sub-point (a) applies wherever the engine has bleeds [CS-E 690(a)]. Sub-point
(b) applies only if direct cabin use of compressor bleed air is declared
[CS-E 690(b)].

**The (a)(3)(ii) relief is live for this engine.** It applies to the four test
sequences of [[CS-E 740|CS-E 740(c)(3)(iii)]], which is the additional test for
engines with 30-Second and 2-Minute OEI Power ratings [CS-E 740(c)(3)]. This
engine declares 30-Second OEI, 2-Minute OEI and Continuous OEI Power ratings.
[[AMC E 690]] gives the accepted means and ties the justification to the strip
examination specifications of [[CS-E 740|CS-E 740(i)(2)]].

**Bleed air purity connects to the toxic products Hazardous Engine Effect.**
[[CS-E 510|CS-E 510(g)(2)(ii)]] classifies concentration of toxic products in
the engine bleed air for the cabin, sufficient to incapacitate crew or
passengers, as a Hazardous Engine Effect. Under
[[AMC E 510|AMC E 510(3)(d)(iv)]], information on delivery rates and
concentrations of toxic products should be provided to the installer, whether or
not CS-E 690(b) is claimed. CS-E 690(b) is the test route for declaring the air
suitable; CS-E 510 governs the safety consequence either way.

[VERIFY: whether direct use of compressor bleed air in the rotorcraft cabin
pressurisation or ventilation system is to be declared. `engine_profile.md` does
not record it, and it decides whether CS-E 690(b) binds.]

Foreign matter reaching bleed-fed areas is governed separately by
[[CS-E 580]], and the bleed effect on vibration characteristics is part of the
survey required by [[CS-E 650|CS-E 650(e)]].

## Not applicable

- **(a)(1)(i)**, **(a)(3)(i)**, in part — the second sentence of each, which governs an alternate endurance test as per CS-E 740(c)(4). CS-E 740(c)(4) is the turbofan alternate endurance test and is excluded from this vault by scope. The staged provisions in the same sub-points apply.

## Amendment history

Amended at Amendment 8. The change added the alternate endurance test route
throughout sub-point (a), and removed the wording that had assumed a single
schedule.

The opening sentence lost two phrases:

- **Before:** "For an Engine having bleed(s) for aircraft and/or Engine uses, the standard Engine endurance test schedule of CS-E 740 must be varied in accordance with this paragraph CS-E 690(a)"
- **After:** "For an Engine having bleed(s) for aircraft and/or Engine uses, the Engine endurance test schedule of CS-E 740 must be varied in accordance with CS-E 690(a)"

Deleting "standard" is the substantive part. It removed the assumption that one
endurance schedule exists, which is what made room for the alternate test. The
deletion of "this paragraph" is editorial.

Sub-point (a)(1)(i) gained the qualifier "as per CS-E 740(c)(1), (2) or (3)" on
the existing sentence, and then a new sentence for the alternate test: "If the
applicant uses an alternate endurance test as per CS-E 740(c)(4), which does not
have a prescribed number of test stages, the bleed controls must be exercised 25
times in evenly distributed intervals."

Sub-point (a)(3)(i) gained "If the applicant uses an endurance test as per
CS-E 740(c)(1), (2) or (3)" before the stage list, and a matching second
sentence requiring the bleeds to be in operation for one fifth of the test under
the alternate endurance test.

Sub-point (a)(2) gained one word: it read "Include a calibration with each bleed
in operation separately" and now reads "Include a calibration test with each
bleed in operation separately".

For this engine the amendment changes nothing in substance. The inserted
material governs the turbofan alternate endurance test of CS-E 740(c)(4), which
is out of scope, and the staged obligations that apply here are unchanged apart
from the explicit sub-point references. The same amendment added
[[CS-E 740|CS-E 740(c)(4)]] to CS-E 740 and amended [[AMC E 690]].

The paragraph carries `[Amdt. No.: E/1]` and `[Amdt No: E/8]`.
