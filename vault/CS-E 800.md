# CS-E 800 — Bird Strike and Ingestion

> [!summary]
> The engine must respond safely to specified bird encounters, as part of
> CS-E 540 compliance. A single large bird is ingested at the rotorcraft's
> maximum normal-operation airspeed and must not cause a Hazardous Engine Effect.
> Larger threats and the medium and small bird tests are gated by inlet throat
> area, and a multi-engine rotorcraft engine is expressly relieved from the
> medium and small bird specifications, at the price of an endorsed approval. The
> inlet throat area itself becomes a published installation limitation.

## Requirement

### Objective and the single large bird

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Demonstrate that the engine will respond in a safe manner following specified encounters with birds, as part of the compliance with CS-E 540. The demonstration addresses the ingestion of large, medium and small birds, and the effect of the impact of such birds upon the front of the engine. | Required |
| **(b)** | Carry out an engine ingestion test using a large bird. Alternative evidence may be acceptable as provided under CS-E 800(g)(1). | Required |
| **(b)(1)(i)** | Stabilise the engine operating conditions prior to ingestion at not less than 100 % of the Take-off Power at the test day ambient conditions. | Required |
| **(b)(1)(i)** | Account in the demonstration of compliance for engine operation at sea level take-off conditions on the hottest day that a minimum engine can achieve maximum rated Take-off Power. | Required |
| **(b)(1)(ii)(A)** | Use a bird of a minimum mass of 1.85 kg for engine inlet throat areas of less than 1.35 m², unless a smaller bird is determined to be a more severe demonstration. | Required |
| **(b)(1)(ii)(B)** | 2.75 kg for engine inlet throat areas of less than 3.90 m² but equal to or greater than 1.35 m². | Required |
| **(b)(1)(ii)(C)** | 3.65 kg for engine inlet throat areas equal to or greater than 3.90 m². | Required |
| **(b)(1)(iii)** | Aim the bird at the most critical exposed location on the first stage rotor blades. | Required |
| **(b)(1)(iv)** | Use a bird speed of the maximum airspeed for normal flight operations for engines to be installed on Rotorcraft. | Required |
| **(b)(1)(v)** | Permit no power lever movement within 15 seconds following the ingestion. | Required |
| **(b)(2)** | Ingestion of this single large bird must not result in a Hazardous Engine Effect. | Required |

Sub-point (b)(1)(iv) is the one place where CS-E gives the aeroplane and the
rotorcraft different numbers in the same sentence: "A bird speed of 200 knots for
Engines to be installed on aeroplanes or the maximum airspeed for normal flight
operations for Engines to be installed on Rotorcraft" [CS-E 800(b)(1)(iv)]. The
aeroplane figure is retained here because it defines the rotorcraft case by
contrast — the rotorcraft speed is not a fixed number but a declared one.

The acceptance criterion in (b)(2) is the most permissive in the paragraph.
[[AMC E 800|AMC E 800(1)(a)(iii)]] makes it explicit: "The complete loss of power
or thrust is acceptable after the ingestion of the single large bird." What must
not happen is a Hazardous Engine Effect.

The bird mass tiers in (b)(1)(ii) are set by inlet throat area, with an escape in
(A) that a smaller bird may be required where it is more severe.

### Impact on the front of the engine

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(f)** | Evaluate the impact against the front of the engine of the largest medium bird required by CS-E 800(d)(1)(v)(A) and of the large bird required by CS-E 800(b)(1)(ii), for compliance with CS-E 540, under the engine conditions specified for the ingestion tests. | Required |
| **(f)** | Use as the bird speed the critical bird ingestion speed for the critical locations within the range of airspeeds for normal flight operations up to 450 m (1 500 ft) above ground level, not higher than the speeds for the ingestion tests. | Required |
| **(f)** | The impact evaluation may be carried out separately from the ingestion evaluation. | Permitted |
| **(f)** | Assess any damage resulting from the impact on the front of the engine in relation to consequential damage on the rotating blades. | Required |

Impact and ingestion are different events. [[AMC E 800|AMC E 800(3)(a)]] defines
the front of the engine as "any part of the Engine which can be struck by a
bird", and [[AMC E 800|AMC E 800(3)(b)]] defines ingestion as "the passage of a
bird into the rotating blades". Sub-point (f) covers what the bird hits without
entering.

### General provisions

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(g)(1)** | Perform engine tests as required under CS-E 800(b), (c), (d) and (e), unless it is agreed that alternative evidence — engine tests, rig tests, analysis or an appropriate combination — may come from the applicant's experience with engines of comparable size, design, construction, performance and handling characteristics, obtained during development, certification or operation. | Required |
| **(g)(2)** | The engine test described in CS-E 800(b)(1), with regard to the single large bird, may be waived if it can be shown by test or analysis that the specifications of CS-E 810(a) are more severe. | Relief |
| **(g)(4)** | Limit exceedances may be permitted to occur during the tests of CS-E 800(c), (d) and (e). Record any limit exceedance and show it to be acceptable under CS-E 700. | Permitted |
| **(g)(5)** | For an engine that incorporates an inlet protection device, establish compliance with CS-E 800 with the device functioning, and endorse the engine approval accordingly. | Required if claimed |
| **(g)(6)** | If compliance with all of the specifications of CS-E 800 is not established, the engine approval will be endorsed accordingly, by restricting the engine installations to those where birds cannot strike the engine or be ingested by the engine or adversely restrict the airflow into the engine. | Statement |
| **(g)(7)** | An engine to be installed in a multi-Engine Rotorcraft does not need to comply with the medium- or small-bird specifications of CS-E 800(d), but the engine approval will be endorsed accordingly. | Relief |
| **(g)(8)** | Establish the engine inlet throat area, as used in CS-E 800 to determine the bird quantity and mass, and identify it as a limitation on the inlet throat area in the instructions for installation. | Required |

Sub-point (g)(8) turns a test input into a permanent installation limitation. The
inlet throat area that selected the bird masses is published, so an installation
with a different inlet is outside the approval.

The waiver in (g)(2) routes through [[CS-E 810]]: if the blade failure
specification is shown to be more severe, the single large bird engine test may
be waived.

## Compliance

- Engine inlet throat area, established and published as an installation limitation in the instructions for installation of [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]] [CS-E 800(g)(8)].
- Declared maximum airspeed for normal flight operations of the intended rotorcraft, as the large bird test speed [CS-E 800(b)(1)(iv)].
- Single large bird ingestion test at not less than 100 % Take-off Power, with the bird aimed at the most critical exposed location and no power lever movement for 15 seconds [CS-E 800(b)(1)].
- Analysis substantiating the most critical exposed location [CS-E 800(b)(1)(iii)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 800|AMC E 800(1)(a)(i)]].
- Hottest-day minimum-engine account in the compliance demonstration [CS-E 800(b)(1)(i)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 800|AMC E 800(4)(b)]].
- Impact evaluation on the front of the engine, with consequential damage to rotating blades assessed [CS-E 800(f)].
- Alternative evidence from comparable engines, where agreed, within a 10 % critical impact parameter variation [CS-E 800(g)(1)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 800|AMC E 800(4)(c)]].
- Inlet protection device functioning during the demonstration, with the approval endorsed, where one is incorporated [CS-E 800(g)(5)].
- Record of any limit exceedance during the tests, shown acceptable under [[CS-E 700]] [CS-E 800(g)(4)].
- Test facility calibration and tolerance analysis against the critical impact parameter [AMC E 800(2)(a)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 800|AMC E 800(2)(a)]].
- Engine interface data on expected interaction with aircraft systems during ingestion events, in the instructions for installation [AMC E 800(2)(e)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 800|AMC E 800(2)(e)]].

## Application to this engine

**The multi-engine relief applies.** `engine_profile.md` declares a rotorcraft
turboshaft in a multi-engine installation, so CS-E 800(g)(7) applies
directly: the engine "does not need to comply with the medium- or small-bird
specifications of CS-E 800(d), but the Engine approval will be endorsed
accordingly". That removes the medium and small bird ingestion tests, their
schedules and Table A from this programme. The endorsement is the price, and it
is recorded on the approval.

The single large bird test of (b) is **not** relieved, and neither is the impact
evaluation of (f). The (f) evaluation refers to "the largest medium bird required
by CS-E 800(d)(1)(v)(A)", which under the (g)(7) relief is not required, so only
the large bird limb of (f) reaches this engine.

[VERIFY: how CS-E 800(f) applies where CS-E 800(g)(7) has relieved the engine
from CS-E 800(d). The impact evaluation names both the largest medium bird and
the large bird, and the medium bird is no longer determined. The large bird
impact evaluation is unaffected.]

**The test speed is declared, not fixed.** For a rotorcraft engine the bird speed
is "the maximum airspeed for normal flight operations" [CS-E 800(b)(1)(iv)],
which comes from the installation rather than from CS-E.

[VERIFY: the maximum airspeed for normal flight operations of the intended
rotorcraft is not recorded in `engine_profile.md`. It sets the large bird test
speed, and it is also the quantity that AMC E 540(1) asks about when considering
operation above 200 knots for the Extremely Remote bird strike criterion.]

**The inlet throat area governs two paragraphs and is not recorded.** It selects
the bird mass here under (b)(1)(ii), it selects the hailstone count under
[[CS-E 790|CS-E 790(a)(1)]], and under (g)(8) it becomes a published installation
limitation. The same `[VERIFY]` is open in [[CS-E 790]].

**OEI ratings are excluded from the (d) tests anyway.**
[[AMC E 800|AMC E 800(4)(a)]] states that "Any OEI ratings do not have to be
taken into account for compliance with CS-E 800(d)". With (g)(7) relieving (d)
entirely, the point does not arise, but it confirms that the bird tests are run
against the all-engines-operating ratings.

**Load device representativeness matters for a turboshaft.**
[[AMC E 800|AMC E 800(2)(d)]] addresses turboshaft engines tested on an
alternative load device: the interface with the test facility and aircraft
systems should be monitored during the test and used to determine how the engine
would respond in a representative installation.

## Not applicable

- **(c)**, including **(c)(1)** and **(c)(2)** — the large flocking bird test. It applies to engines with an inlet throat area equal to or greater than 2.5 m², and its test schedule, acceptance criteria and bird mass table are written in Rated Take-off Thrust terms.
- **(d)**, including **(d)(1)**, **(d)(2)** and **(d)(3)** — the medium and small bird ingestion tests, their test schedules, Table A and the additional integrity assessment. CS-E 800(g)(7) relieves an engine to be installed in a multi-engine rotorcraft from these specifications, and this engine is declared for a multi-engine rotorcraft installation. The approval is endorsed accordingly.
- **(d)(1)(iii)** — the medium and small bird test schedule for engines other than rotorcraft engines, expressly excluded by its own opening words.
- **(e)**, including **(e)(1)** to **(e)(8)** — the core engine flocking bird ingestion test, which applies to turbofan engines.
- **(g)(3)** — the alternatives to an engine test for showing compliance with CS-E 800(c), which does not apply to this engine.
- **(f)**, in part — the V1 minimum floor for engines to be installed on aeroplanes. The upper bound in the same sentence applies and is carried.
- **(b)(1)(iv)**, in part — the 200-knot aeroplane bird speed. Quoted once in the prose below the Requirement table, not cut, because it defines the rotorcraft case by contrast.
