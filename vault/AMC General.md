# AMC General

> [!quote] AMC General
> "AMC-20 may also provide acceptable means of compliance to the specifications … of this CS-E."

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| — | AMC-20 may supply an acceptable means of compliance for a CS-E specification, in addition to the AMC E paragraphs. | Permitted |

CS-E contains two kinds of paragraph. A `CS-E nnn` paragraph states a
specification. An `AMC E nnn` paragraph states an acceptable means of complying
with it.

This note records that the AMC E paragraphs are not the only source of acceptable
means. AMC-20 is a separate EASA document and may also supply one.

## Compliance

- When proposing a compliance method, search AMC-20 as well as the AMC E paragraphs before concluding that no acceptable means exists [AMC General].

## Application to this engine

Nothing specific to a turboshaft. The provision applies to every CS-E
specification.

AMC-20 is not held in `source/`. Obtain it separately if a compliance method
relies on it.
