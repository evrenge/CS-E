# AMC General

> [!quote] Rule text
> "In addition to the acceptable means of compliance in Book 2 of these Certification Specifications, AMC-20 may also provide acceptable means of compliance to the specifications in Book 1 of this CS-E." — AMC General

## What it means
CS-E has two books. Book 1 holds the specifications (the CS paragraphs). Book 2 holds the accepted means of compliance (the AMC paragraphs).

Book 2 is not the only source of accepted means. AMC-20 is a separate EASA document, and it can also supply an accepted means for a Book 1 specification.

So when Book 2 has no AMC for a specification, check AMC-20 before concluding that no accepted means exists.

## What we must do
- When proposing a compliance method, check AMC-20 as well as Book 2. Accepted method [AMC General].

## Turboshaft note
None.

## Specification
Applies across Book 1. Not tied to a single CS paragraph.
