# CS-E 1000 — General

> [!summary]
> This paragraph sets how Subpart F binds. Compliance with CS-E 1010 and
> CS-E 1020 may be mandatory, depending on what CS-34 references. Compliance with
> the other paragraphs of the subpart is optional and happens only at the
> applicant's request. Whatever is complied with is recorded in notes in the
> engine type certificate data sheet.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| — | Compliance with the specifications of CS-E 1010 and CS-E 1020 may be mandatory for engine type certification, depending on the specifications referenced under CS-34. | Statement |
| — | Compliance with all or some of the other specifications of this subpart is optional, at the request of the applicant. | Statement |
| — | Compliance with the specifications of this subpart will be recorded in notes in the engine type certificate data sheet. | Statement |

CS-E 1000 is a single unnumbered paragraph, so the `Ref` cells carry no sub-point
identifier.

The paragraph imposes no action of its own. It fixes the status of every other
paragraph in Subpart F, and it does so in two tiers. The emissions and fuel
venting paragraphs take their force from CS-34, which sits outside CS-E
altogether. Everything else in the subpart is elective, and nothing in it binds
until the applicant asks for it [CS-E 1000].

The recording sentence applies to every Subpart F paragraph complied with. Once
compliance is shown, it appears as a note in the type certificate data sheet
(TCDS), so an elective claim becomes visible on the certificate [CS-E 1000].

## Compliance

- Statement of which Subpart F paragraphs the applicant elects to comply with [CS-E 1000].
- Determination of whether CS-34 makes [[CS-E 1010]] and [[CS-E 1020]] mandatory for this certification [CS-E 1000].
- TCDS notes recording compliance with each Subpart F paragraph complied with [CS-E 1000]. For emissions the note format is given in [[AMC E 1020|AMC E 1020(1)]].

## Application to this engine

The paragraph applies. It imposes nothing of its own, and it governs the three
other Subpart F paragraphs in scope for this vault: [[CS-E 1010]], [[CS-E 1020]]
and [[CS-E 1050]].

Of the elective paragraphs, only [[CS-E 1050]] is in scope here. The other two
elective paragraphs are excluded, and for reasons that fit the pattern this
paragraph describes: CS-E 1030, Time Limited Dispatch, is not claimed in
`engine_profile.md`, and CS-E 1040, ETOPS, is an aeroplane operation excluded from
this vault by scope. Both are elective under CS-E 1000, so declining them is the
normal operation of the paragraph rather than an omission.

The text of CS-34 settles less than this paragraph implies. CS 34.1 and
CS 34.2 are one sentence each, and each one refers the applicant to point
21.A.21 of Part 21 [ext CS 34.1], [ext CS 34.2]. Neither carries a venting
limit, an emission limit or a test. The requirements are designated by the
Agency under point 21.B.85 [ext GM1 34.1], and the chain ends at ICAO Annex 16.
It is set out in [[CS 34.1]] and [[CS 34.2]].

Therefore, "depending on the specifications referenced under CS-34" resolves to
the Agency's designation under point 21.B.85(a), not to a CS-34 amendment. The
fuel venting obligation reaches this installation through [[CS-E 1010]]. Whether
the emissions side reaches a turboshaft at all is open and is recorded in
[[CS 34.2]].

CS-34 was repealed in May 2025, weeks after CS-E Amendment 8 was issued.
The repeal removes the signpost, not the obligation: see [[CS 34.1]].
