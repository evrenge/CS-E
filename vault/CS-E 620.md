# CS-E 620 — Performance Correction

> [!summary]
> All performance results are corrected to standard sea-level conditions: 1013.25
> hPa, 288 K, and dry air where the correction is significant. Humidity is
> treated differently. No humidity correction may be made to the power obtained,
> but humidity corrections for high atmospheric temperatures at altitudes up to
> 4 500 m must still be established for each turbine engine type, for the
> aircraft performance assessment.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Correct all performance results to the assumed conditions of atmospheric pressure and temperature at mean sea level given in (a)(1) to (a)(3). | Required |
| **(a)(1)** | Pressure: 1013.25 hPa. | Statement |
| **(a)(2)** | Temperature: 288 K. | Statement |
| **(a)(3)** | Atmosphere: dry air, if correction is significant. | Statement |
| **(b)** | Make no correction for humidity of the air supply to the power obtained. | Required |
| **(b)** | Establish humidity corrections appropriate to high atmospheric temperatures, at altitudes up to 4 500 m, for each type of turbine engine, for use in the assessment of aircraft performance in these conditions. | Required |

Sub-point (b) contains two duties that pull in opposite directions and must not
be merged. Humidity may **not** be used to correct declared power — the engine's
performance is stated on dry air. Humidity corrections must nonetheless be
**established**, for the aircraft performance assessment, at high atmospheric
temperatures up to 4 500 m.

The effect is to keep the engine rating conservative while still giving the
installer the data to predict real aircraft performance in hot, humid air.

The dry-air condition in (a)(3) is qualified: it applies "if correction is
significant" [CS-E 620(a)(3)].

## Compliance

- Performance results corrected to 1013.25 hPa and 288 K, on dry air where the correction is significant [CS-E 620(a)]. Accepted means: the formulae of [[AMC E 620]].
- Evidence that no humidity correction was applied to the power obtained [CS-E 620(b)].
- Established humidity corrections for high atmospheric temperatures at altitudes up to 4 500 m, for this engine type [CS-E 620(b)], delivered to the installer in the manuals of [[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]].

## Application to this engine

Both sub-points apply. Neither is restricted by engine type or rating.

The correction applies to every declared rating, so the humidity corrections
under (b) cover the OEI ratings as well as the all-engines-operating ratings.
That matters for a rotorcraft, where the OEI power available in hot and humid
conditions is what the installer needs for the [[AMC E 20|AMC E 20(f)]] power
assurance data.

The 4 500 m ceiling in (b) bounds the humidity work. It is an altitude limit on
the correction data, not on engine operation.

The declared ratings of [[CS-E 40|CS-E 40(c)]] are already established on standard
atmospheric conditions without aircraft bleed, so CS-E 620 and CS-E 40(c) fix the
same reference atmosphere from two directions: CS-E 40(c) for the rating basis,
CS-E 620 for the test results that substantiate it.

[[CS-E 730]] is the calibration test that produces the performance results this
paragraph corrects.
