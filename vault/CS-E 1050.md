# CS-E 1050 — Exposure to volcanic cloud hazards

> [!summary]
> The paragraph has two short specifications. The susceptibility of turbine
> engine features to the effects of volcanic cloud hazards must be established.
> The information necessary for safe operation must be provided in the relevant
> documentation. Compliance is elective under CS-E 1000. Once elected, both
> sub-points bind, and the second makes the result an operator-facing
> deliverable.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Establish the susceptibility of turbine engine features to the effects of volcanic cloud hazards. | Required |
| **(b)** | Provide the information necessary for safe operation in the relevant documentation. | Required |

Compliance with CS-E 1050 is optional. [[CS-E 1000]] makes compliance with the
Subpart F paragraphs other than [[CS-E 1010]] and [[CS-E 1020]] "optional, at
the request of the applicant" [CS-E 1000]. That electiveness is imposed from
outside the paragraph; CS-E 1050's own verb is "must" in both sub-points, so
both rows read Required. The same treatment is applied to [[CS-E 1010]] and
[[CS-E 1020]], whose force is likewise set by CS-E 1000 rather than by their own
wording. Once the applicant requests it, both sub-points are mandatory and
compliance is recorded in a note in the type certificate data sheet (TCDS)
[CS-E 1000].

The paragraph sets no threshold, no ash concentration and no exposure duration.
It requires susceptibility to be **established**, not to be absent. Therefore,
an engine that is susceptible can still comply, by establishing the
susceptibility and telling operators what they need to know.

Sub-point (b) makes the result of (a) a deliverable. The information necessary
for safe operation must reach the relevant documentation [CS-E 1050(b)].

## Compliance

- Susceptibility assessment of the engine features affected by volcanic cloud hazards [CS-E 1050(a)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 1050]].
- Identification of the susceptible features and the nature and severity of the effects [CS-E 1050(a)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 1050|AMC E 1050(1)]], [[AMC E 1050|AMC E 1050(2)]].
- Pre-flight, in-flight and post-flight precautions for the operator, with any necessary amendments to the engine manuals, Dispatch Deviation, or equivalents [CS-E 1050(b)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 1050|AMC E 1050(3)]].
- Recommended continued airworthiness inspections for operations in volcanic cloud contaminated airspace [CS-E 1050(b)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 1050|AMC E 1050(4)]], within [[CS-E 25]].
- TCDS note recording compliance. [[CS-E 1000]] states that compliance with the specifications of Subpart F will be recorded in notes in the engine type certificate data sheet [CS-E 1000].

## Application to this engine

The paragraph applies if the applicant elects it. Nothing in it is restricted by
engine type, rating or control system.

The susceptible features that [[AMC E 1050|AMC E 1050(1)]] lists are already
covered elsewhere in the vault, which reduces the marginal work of electing this
paragraph. [[AMC E 1050]] maps each of the six named feature categories onto the
paragraph that already requires the work.

The documentation duty under (b) lands in the same manuals as the continued
airworthiness material of [[CS-E 25]] and the installation information of
[[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]].
