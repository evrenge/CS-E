# CS-E 1050 — Exposure to volcanic cloud hazards

> [!summary]
> Two short specifications. The susceptibility of turbine engine features to the
> effects of volcanic cloud hazards must be established, and the information an
> operator needs for safe operation must be provided in the relevant
> documentation. Compliance is elective under CS-E 1000, but once elected both
> sub-points bind and the second makes the result an operator-facing deliverable.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Establish the susceptibility of turbine engine features to the effects of volcanic cloud hazards. | Required if claimed |
| **(b)** | Provide the information necessary for safe operation in the relevant documentation. | Required if claimed |

Compliance with CS-E 1050 is optional. [[CS-E 1000]] makes compliance with the
Subpart F paragraphs other than [[CS-E 1010]] and [[CS-E 1020]] "optional, at the
request of the applicant" [CS-E 1000], which is why both rows read Required if
claimed rather than Required. Once the applicant requests it, both sub-points are
mandatory and compliance is recorded in a TCDS note.

The paragraph sets no threshold, no ash concentration and no exposure duration.
It requires susceptibility to be **established**, not to be absent, so an engine
that is susceptible can still comply — by establishing the susceptibility and
telling operators what they need to know.

Sub-point (b) is the deliverable. The output of (a) is of no use inside the
applicant's organisation alone; it has to reach the relevant documentation.

## Compliance

- Susceptibility assessment of the engine features affected by volcanic cloud hazards [CS-E 1050(a)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 1050]] — a combination of experience, studies, analysis and testing of parts, sub-assemblies or engines.
- Identification of the susceptible features and the nature and severity of the effects [CS-E 1050(a)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 1050|AMC E 1050(1)]], [[AMC E 1050|AMC E 1050(2)]].
- Pre-flight, in-flight and post-flight precautions for the operator, with any necessary amendments to the engine manuals or dispatch deviation documents [CS-E 1050(b)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 1050|AMC E 1050(3)]].
- Recommended continued airworthiness inspections for operations in volcanic cloud contaminated airspace [CS-E 1050(b)]. Accepted means: [[AMC E 1050|AMC E 1050(4)]], within [[CS-E 25]].
- TCDS note recording compliance, as required by [[CS-E 1000]].

## Application to this engine

The paragraph applies if the applicant elects it. Nothing in it is restricted by
engine type, rating or control system.

Several of the susceptible features that [[AMC E 1050|AMC E 1050(1)]] lists are
already covered elsewhere in the vault, which reduces the marginal work of
electing this paragraph. Erosion of compressor blades and internal parts relates
to the foreign matter provisions of [[CS-E 540]] and the bleed air protection of
[[CS-E 580]]. Loss of surge margin, engine stall, flame out and inability to
restart relate to [[CS-E 500|CS-E 500(a)]] and to the relighting tests of
[[CS-E 910]]. Corrosion relates to [[CS-E 90]]. Oil and fuel circuit contamination
relates to [[CS-E 560]] and [[CS-E 570]]. Electrical, hydraulic and pneumatic
systems relate to [[CS-E 80]] and, for a full-authority EECS, to [[CS-E 50]].

The documentation duty under (b) lands in the same manuals as the continued
airworthiness material of [[CS-E 25]] and the installation information of
[[CS-E 20|CS-E 20(d)]].
