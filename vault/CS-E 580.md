# CS-E 580 — Air Systems

> [!summary]
> Where bleed air cools or pressurises an engine area whose function could be
> harmed by foreign matter, the design must stop foreign matter of unacceptable
> quantity or unacceptable size from passing. The specification is narrow in
> trigger but absolute in effect: it applies only to bleed-fed areas that are
> sensitive to ingress, and for those it requires the passage to be precluded.

## Requirement

| Ref | Obligation | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| — | Where bleed air is used to cool or to pressurise areas of the engine whose functions could be detrimentally affected by the ingress of foreign matter, such as sand or dust, design the system so that the passage of foreign matter of unacceptable quantity or unacceptable size is precluded. | Required |

CS-E 580 is a single unnumbered sentence, so the `Ref` cell carries no sub-point
identifier.

Two thresholds appear and they are separate: unacceptable **quantity** and
unacceptable **size** [CS-E 580]. A filtration argument that addresses only
particle size does not answer the quantity limb, and vice versa.

The obligation is to preclude passage, not to tolerate it [CS-E 580]. What
counts as unacceptable is set by the function that could be affected, so the
applicant has to identify the sensitive areas first.

Sand and dust are given as examples, introduced by "e.g.", so the foreign matter
population is not limited to them.

## Compliance

- Identification of the engine areas cooled or pressurised by bleed air whose functions could be detrimentally affected by foreign matter ingress [CS-E 580].
- Definition of the unacceptable quantity and unacceptable size for each such area, derived from the function at risk [CS-E 580].
- Design substantiation that passage of such foreign matter is precluded [CS-E 580].

## Application to this engine

The paragraph applies. It has no AMC of its own.

Sand and dust are the named examples [CS-E 580]. The related environmental
qualification is the sand and dust item in [[AMC E 80|AMC E 80(2)(a)]], which
applies "to all equipment that is not environmentally sealed" and is tested to a
named standard. CS-E 580 is a different duty: it protects internal engine areas
through the bleed air path rather than qualifying equipment against an external
environment.

CS-E 580 sits alongside two neighbouring specifications and does not duplicate
either. [[CS-E 540]] governs strike and ingestion of foreign matter into the
engine as a whole, and [[CS-E 690]] governs engine bleed. Turbine cooling is the
clearest case of a bleed-fed function whose loss matters. [[CS-E 60|CS-E 60(e)]]
requires instrumentation for the flight crew to monitor the turbine cooling
system, unless one of three exemptions is evidenced.
