(iii) 
The following test sequence must be performed four times for a total time of not 
less than 120 minutes. If a stop occurs during this test, the interrupted sequence 
must be repeated unless it is shown that the severity of the test is not reduced if it 
were continued: 
Part 1: Three minutes at Take-off Power.  
Part 2: Thirty seconds at 30-Second OEI Power.  
Part 3: Two minutes at 2-Minute OEI Power.  
Part 4: Five minutes at whichever is the greatest of, as applicable, 30-Minute OEI 
Power, Continuous OEI Power and Maximum Continuous Power, except that 
during the first test sequence this period must be sixty-five minutes. 
However, where the greatest is the 30-Minute OEI Power, that sixty-five 
minutes period must consist of thirty minutes at 30-Minute OEI Power 
followed by thirty-five minutes at whichever is the greater of Continuous OEI 
Power and Maximum Continuous Power, as applicable.
