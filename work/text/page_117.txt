CS-E 640 Pressure Loads 
(See AMC E 640) 
(a) 
Static Pressure Loads 
It must be established by test, validated analysis or combination thereof that all static parts 
which are subject to significant gas or liquid pressure loads will not, for a stabilised period of 
one minute:  
(1) 
Exhibit permanent distortion beyond serviceable limits or exhibit leakage which could 
result in a Hazardous Engine Effect when subjected to the greater of the following 
pressures: 
(i) 
1.1 times the maximum working pressure or, 
(ii) 
1.33 times the normal working pressure or, 
(iii) 
35 kPa above the normal working pressure, and 
(2) 
Exhibit fracture or burst when subjected to the greater of the following pressures: 
(i) 
1·15 times the maximum possible pressure or, 
(ii) 
1·5 times the maximum working pressure or, 
(iii) 
35 kPa above the maximum possible pressure.
