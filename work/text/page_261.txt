CS-E 1040 ETOPS 
(See AMC 20-6) 
In order to be approved for ETOPS capability, the engine shall achieve an IFSD rate that is compatible 
with the safety target associated to the maximum flight duration and the longest diversion time for 
which approval is being sought.
