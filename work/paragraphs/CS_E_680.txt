# CS-E 680 Inclination and Gyroscopic Load Effects
# id: CS-E 680
# subpart: E
# pages: 133-133
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

(See AMC E 680)
It must be demonstrated that the effects of inclination on Engine running are not seriously detrimental
and that the Engine is designed to withstand the gyroscopic loads resulting from normal flight
manoeuvres.
[Amdt. No.: E/1]
