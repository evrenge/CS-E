# CS-E 525 Continued Rotation
# id: CS-E 525
# subpart: D
# pages: 108-109
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

(see AMC E 525)
If any of the Engine’s main rotating systems will continue to rotate after the Engine is shutdown for
any reason while in flight, and means to prevent that continued rotation, are not provided, any
continued rotation during the maximum period of flight and in the flight conditions expected to occur
with that Engine inoperative must not result in effects that would be unacceptable under CS-E 510.
