# CS-E 90 Prevention of Corrosion and Deterioration
# id: CS-E 90
# subpart: A
# pages: 50-50
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

(a)
Each Engine component and each item of equipment must be protected from corrosion and
deterioration in an approved manner.
(b)
Materials which will render the Engine inherently self-protecting against corrosion, without the
use of internal and external corrosion inhibitors, must be used wherever possible.
