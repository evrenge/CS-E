# AMC E 520(a) Strength - High Cycle Fatigue
# id: AMC E 520(a)
# subpart: D
# pages: 106-107
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

In order to minimise the adverse consequences of Failures due to unpredicted high cycle fatigue it is
recommended that, normally, the relative fatigue strengths of the blade/disc are graded such that
they lie in the ascending order: blade form, blade root, disc blade attachment, disc rim.
