# CS-E 1020 Engine Emissions
# id: CS-E 1020
# subpart: F
# pages: 252-252
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

(See AMC E 1020)
It must be demonstrated, by test or analysis or combination thereof, that the Engine type design
complies with the emission specifications of CS 34.2 in effect at date of Engine certification. The
resulting data must be recorded.
