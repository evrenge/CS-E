# CS-E 350 Calibration Tests
# id: CS-E 350
# subpart: C
# pages: 72-72
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

(See AMC E 350)
(a)
The power characteristics of the Engine must be established, under all normal operating
conditions within the declared flight envelope, by means of sufficient calibration testing.
(b)
In order to identify the Engine power changes that may occur during the endurance test of CS-
E 440, sea level power calibration curves of the test engine shall be established at the beginning
and the end of the endurance test
