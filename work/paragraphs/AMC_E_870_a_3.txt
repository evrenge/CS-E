# AMC E 870(a)(3) Exhaust Gas Over-temperature Test
# id: AMC E 870(a)(3)
# subpart: E
# pages: 240-240
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

In order to comply with CS-E 870(a)(3), it should be shown that an over-temperature event does not
compromise the ability of the Engine to reach its Rated 30-Second/2-Minute OEI Power.
[Amdt. No.: E/1]
