# CS-E 70 Materials and Manufacturing Methods
# id: CS-E 70
# subpart: A
# pages: 41-41
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

(See AMC E 70)
(a)
The suitability and durability of materials used in the Engine must be established on the basis
of experience or tests. The assumed design values of properties of materials must be suitably
related to the minimum properties stated in the material specification.
(b)
Manufacturing methods and processes must be such as to produce sound structure and
mechanisms which retain the original mechanical properties under reasonable service
conditions.
