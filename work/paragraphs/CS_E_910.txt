# CS-E 910 Relighting In Flight
# id: CS-E 910
# subpart: E
# pages: 243-244
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

The Engine constructor must recommend an envelope of conditions for Engine relighting in flight, and
must substantiate it by appropriate tests or other evidence. The recommendation must state all the
conditions applicable, e.g. altitude, air speed, Engine windmilling rotational speed, whether starter
assistance is required, the recommended drill.
