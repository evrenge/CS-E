# CS-E 450 Ignition Tests
# id: CS-E 450
# subpart: C
# pages: 79-79
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

For spark-ignition Engines:
(a)
If the design of the ignition system includes redundancy, the reduction in Engine power resulting
from loss of redundancy shall be established. Tests shall be carried out with the Engine running
at Take-off power setting at the beginning and at the end of each part of the endurance test of
CS-E 440.
(b)
In no case shall the reduction in power during the test exceed the value declared under CS-E
240(b)(1).
