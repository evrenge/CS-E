# AMC E 1000 Environmental and Operational Design Specifications - General
# id: AMC E 1000
# subpart: F
# pages: 252-252
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

General
CS-E subpart F defines specifications for the design of an engine to determine a configuration which
would be required as part of the approval for particular aircraft operations such as ETOPS or Time
Limited Dispatch.
It also defines the specifications necessary at the engine level for compliance with CS-34.
