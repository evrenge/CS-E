# AMC E 150(a) Tests - General conduct of tests
# id: AMC E 150(a)
# subpart: A
# pages: 61-61
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

For piston Engines, where the operating conditions of a test represent Maximum Continuous Power
at altitude, a higher grade fuel or any other approved anti-detonant may be used if such is required to
suppress detonation during the test.
[Amdt. No.: E/1]
