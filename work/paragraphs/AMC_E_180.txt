# AMC E 180 Propeller Functioning Tests
# id: AMC E 180
# subpart: A
# pages: 64-64
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

For Propeller approval the remaining tests of CS-P may be conducted on another Engine of the same
type providing the same Propeller without further adjustment is used.
For Piston Engines, auxiliary cooling may be used during tests of CS-E 180(b)(3) and (4).
