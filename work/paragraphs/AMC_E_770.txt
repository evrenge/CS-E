# AMC E 770 Low Temperature Starting Tests
# id: AMC E 770
# subpart: E
# pages: 176-176
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

It is normally acceptable in each of the tests of CS-E 770(b) and (c) to allow the Engine to inspire
uncooled intake air.
The Constructor may declare a Minimum oil temperature which, with any associated conditions, may
be used for the purpose of opening up from ground idle for warming up or taxying.
[Amdt. No.: E/1]
