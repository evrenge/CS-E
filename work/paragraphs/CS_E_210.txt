# CS-E 210 Failure Analysis
# id: CS-E 210
# subpart: B
# pages: 65-65
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

(See AMC E 210)
(a)
A Failure analysis of the Engine, including the control system for a typical installation must be
made to establish that no single Fault, or double Fault if one of the Faults may be present and
undetected during pre-flight checks, could lead to unsafe Engine conditions beyond the normal
control of the flight crew.
(b)
In certain cases the Failure analysis will depend on assumed installation conditions. Such
assumptions must be stated in the analysis.
