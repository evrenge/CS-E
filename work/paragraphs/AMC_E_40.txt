# AMC E 40 Ratings
# id: AMC E 40
# subpart: A
# pages: 30-30
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

The thrust and / or power ratings to be approved should be adequately justified by the applicant, using
as appropriate the results of the calibration tests (CS-E 350 or CS-E 730) and the values substantiated
by the endurance test (CS-E 440 or CS-E 740) or other means.
[Amdt. No.: E/1]
