# AMC E 680 Inclination and Gyroscopic Load Effects
# id: AMC E 680
# subpart: E
# pages: 133-133
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

Normally inclination and gyroscopic load effects will be proved during flight testing but in certain cases
some supplementary running or rig testing, to an agreed schedule, may be required before flight tests
can be permitted.
[Amdt. No.: E/1]
