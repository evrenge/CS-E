# AMC E 380 Low Temperature Starting Tests
# id: AMC E 380
# subpart: C
# pages: 74-74
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

The minimum tropical and temperate sea-level temperature as defined in CS-Definitions is -20°C. In
order not to restrict operation of the aircraft in which the Engine is installed, it is recommended that
the minimum temperature declared for starting should be not higher than -20°C.
[Amdt. No.: E/1]
