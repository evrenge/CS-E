# CS-E 120 Identification
# id: CS-E 120
# subpart: A
# pages: 51-51
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

(a)
The Engine identification must comply with points 21.A.801(a) and (b), and point 21.A.805 of
Part 21.
(b)
Major Engine modules that can be changed independently in service must be suitably identified
so as to ensure traceability of parts and to enable proper control over the interchangeability of
such modules with different Engine variants.
