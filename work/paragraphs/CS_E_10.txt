# CS-E 10 Applicability
# id: CS-E 10
# subpart: A
# pages: 15-15
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

(a)
This CS-E contains airworthiness specifications for the issue of type certificates, and changes to
those certificates, for Engines, in accordance with Part 21.
(b)
CS-E contains the specifications for the approval for use of the Engine with a thrust reverser, if
fitted. If compliance is shown, the defined thrust reverser approved for use will be noted in the
Engine certification documentation. Otherwise, the documentation will be endorsed to indicate
that the use of a thrust reverser is prohibited.
(c)
The specifications of Subparts A, B and C apply to Piston Engines. Any necessary variations of
the specifications of Subparts B and C for Piston Engines intended for use in rotorcraft will be
decided in accordance with point 21.B.75 of Part 21.
(d)
The specifications of subparts A, D, E and F apply to Turbine Engines.
