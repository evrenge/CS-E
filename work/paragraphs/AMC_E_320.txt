# AMC E 320 Performance Correction
# id: AMC E 320
# subpart: C
# pages: 70-71
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

(1)
An acceptable method for calculating performance corrections is defined in ISO 1585:2020,
Road vehicles - Engine test code - Net power.
(2)
Since the effect of free water on power output is within ±1% over the range of water/air ratios
normally encountered in operation, and the amount of free water is exceedingly difficult to
measure, no corrections for free water need to be made.
