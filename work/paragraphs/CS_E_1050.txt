# CS-E 1050 Exposure to volcanic cloud hazards
# id: CS-E 1050
# subpart: F
# pages: 261-261
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

(See AMC E 1050)
(a)
The susceptibility of turbine Engine features to the effects of volcanic cloud hazards must be
established.
(b)
Information necessary for safe operation must be provided in the relevant documentation.
