# AMC E 830(c) Maximum Engine Over-speed
# id: AMC E 830(c)
# subpart: E
# pages: 230-231
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

In order to comply with CS-E 830(c), it should be shown that an over-speed event does not
compromise the ability of the Engine to reach its Rated 30-Second/2-Minute OEI Power.
