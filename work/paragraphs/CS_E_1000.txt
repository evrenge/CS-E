# CS-E 1000 General
# id: CS-E 1000
# subpart: F
# pages: 252-252
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

(See AMC E 1000)
Compliance with the specifications of CS-E 1010 and CS-E 1020 may be mandatory for Engine type
certification depending on the specifications referenced under CS-34. Compliance with all or some of
the other specifications of this subpart is optional, at the request of the applicant.
Compliance with the specifications of this subpart will be recorded in notes in the Engine type
certificate data sheet.
