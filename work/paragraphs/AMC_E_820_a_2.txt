# AMC E 820(a)(2) Over-torque Test
# id: AMC E 820(a)(2)
# subpart: E
# pages: 230-230
# source: CS-E_Amendment_8.pdf

In order to comply with CS-E 820(a)(2), it should be shown that an over-torque event does not
compromise the ability of the Engine to reach its Rated 30-Second/2-Minute OEI Power.
[Amdt No: E/1]
